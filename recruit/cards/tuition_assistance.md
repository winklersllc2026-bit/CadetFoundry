**Tuition Assistance (TA)** pays for **off-duty** college classes, a high school diploma, and some certificate and language classes (para 4-2). **Credentialing Assistance (CA)** pays for civilian credentials — training, study materials and exam fees — whether or not they relate to your MOS. The list of eligible credentials is on **Army COOL** (paras 4-10, 4-11).

**Who can use them** (paras 4-3, 4-12):
- You must **finish BCT** first (Reserve Component Soldiers are exempt from that rule).
- Your record must show a **high school diploma or equivalent** before college TA or CA.
- Reserve and Guard Soldiers must be "satisfactory participants."
- Commissioned officers can't use CA; warrant officers can (para 4-13).

**How much:** the Army publishes the yearly and lifetime limits each year in an **ALARACT message** — ask your education center for the current numbers (para 4-4). TA doesn't pay school fees.

**How to use TA** (para 4-6):
1. Create an **ArmyIgnitED** account and complete the **Virtual Benefits Training** (again every year).
2. First time: do the Army decision support tool assessment and get counseling from an education advisor.
3. Get an **education goal** approved by an education counselor.
4. Request TA for each class in ArmyIgnitED — up to **60 days** before the term starts and **no later than 7 days** before. Request TA **before** registering with the school.
5. Your supervisor or the commander's representative approves it.
6. After two TA-funded classes, you need an approved **Evaluated Degree Plan** to keep using TA.

**Keep it** (para 4-5):
- Keep at least a **2.0 GPA** after 15 undergraduate semester hours (3.0 after 6 graduate hours).
- A grade below **C** (below **B** for graduate classes), or withdrawing with cost to the Army, means the Army takes the money back (**recoupment**).
- **Two** recoupments in one fiscal year suspends TA and CA for **12 months**.

Your installation **education center** is where to start.
