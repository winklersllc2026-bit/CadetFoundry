The **Army Fitness Test (AFT)** has **five events** (AFT Scoring Scales, effective 1 June 2025):
1. **3-repetition maximum deadlift** (pounds)
2. **Hand-release push-ups** (repetitions in 2 minutes)
3. **Sprint / drag / carry** (time)
4. **Plank** (time)
5. **Two-mile run** (time)

There are **no pull-ups** on the AFT.

**To pass** (Army announcement, 21 April 2025): at least **60 points in every event**, and a total of:
- **350** for Soldiers in **combat specialties** (combat standard: sex-neutral, scored by age), or
- **300** for **combat-enabling specialties** (general standard: scored by sex and age).

Each event is worth up to **100 points**. Recruit's AFT scorer (Log → AFT) uses the official tables.
