---
title: Grooming — Shaving and Mustaches
topic: Uniforms
source: AR 670-1, Wear and Appearance of Army Uniforms and Insignia (26 January 2021), para 3-2a(2)(b)
---
**Shave:** males keep their face **clean-shaven** in uniform, and in civilian clothes on duty.

**Mustaches are allowed.** If you wear one, keep it **neatly trimmed, tapered and tidy** — not chopped off or bushy. No part of it may:
- cover the **upper lip line**,
- extend sideways past a vertical line drawn up from the **corners of the mouth**, or
- extend above a line at the **lowest part of the nose**.

**Not authorized:** handlebar mustaches, goatees and beards. A beard is allowed only when appropriate medical authority allows it, and the medical paperwork must give the maximum length.
