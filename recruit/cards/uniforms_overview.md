**Where the rules are:** **AR 670-1** is the regulation — grooming, tattoos, jewelry, and when each uniform may be worn. **DA PAM 670-1** is the how-to, with placements, measurements and figures.

**Army Combat Uniform (ACU)** in the **Operational Camouflage Pattern (OCP)** — the daily work, utility and field uniform (PAM para 4-1). It's made to be worn under body armor (AR para 4-3a).
- Coat, trousers, undershirt, undergarments, belt, socks, boots, headgear (para 4-2).
- **Sleeves rolled up:** neatly above the elbow, but **no more than 3 inches** above it (para 4-3b).
- **Not in off-post places that mainly sell alcohol** to drink on the premises (para 4-3c).

**Army Physical Fitness Uniform (APFU)** — the PT uniform (PAM para 10-1; AR para 10-3):
- Worn on and off duty, on and off the installation, unless the commander restricts it.
- **Not for commercial travel.**
- **Not in off-post businesses, except to buy essentials** such as gas.
- Not for social or official events off the installation, like funerals or weddings.

**Army Green Service Uniform (AGSU)** (para 14-3) and **Army Service Uniform (ASU, the blue uniform)** (para 11-3) — worn on duty when the commander prescribes it, at social functions, and for official events such as memorial services, funerals, weddings and patriotic ceremonies. Both are authorized for commercial travel.

Uniform rules change. If a drill sergeant's guidance differs from this card, follow the drill sergeant and ask Recruit to look up the current paragraph.
