Title: The new phone
Competency: Self-discipline with money
Situation:
You've saved $600 from your summer job, planning to use it for your car insurance this fall. A new phone just came out, and your friends all have it. The store offers a credit card for the phone: "No payment for 12 months!" Your current phone works fine, but the screen is cracked.
Choice: Open the store card and buy the phone today — no payment for a year.
Outcome: You forget about the card for months. The promotion has fine print: since you didn't pay it all off in time, you owe interest back to the purchase date. The bill is much more than the phone's price, and a late payment shows up on your credit report.
Strengths: You got the phone you wanted right away.
Weaknesses: Credit is a loan. Deals like "no payment for 12 months" can cost far more if you don't pay in full by the deadline, and late payments hurt your credit.
Choice: Spend your savings on the phone and figure out insurance later.
Outcome: You have the phone, but when the insurance bill comes due you have to ask your parents for help and take extra shifts.
Strengths: You didn't take on debt.
Weaknesses: You spent money you'd already planned for something more important, and had nothing left for an emergency.
Choice: Wait 24 hours, then fix the cracked screen for much less, keep the rest in savings, and start a separate savings goal for the new phone.
Outcome: The urge to buy fades by the next day. The repair costs a small part of your savings, your insurance is covered, and a few months later you buy a phone with cash you saved for it.
Strengths: You stuck to your plan, avoided interest and debt, and still got what you wanted — later.
Weaknesses: You wait longer than your friends did.
Principles:
- A budget means planning your money before you spend it, and savings come first.
- Pay credit cards in full and on time; paying late or only the minimum costs more.
- Investor.gov's tip for impulse buys: wait 24 hours before buying anything.
Other approaches:
Ask a parent or trusted adult to look over any credit offer with you before you sign — read what happens if you miss the deadline.
Reflection: What's something you wanted badly that you were glad you didn't buy? What helps you wait?
