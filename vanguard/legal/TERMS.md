## 3. Who may use it
- An instructor may install Vanguard and use it **within their own JROTC program**, including making copies for their own Cadets (for example with "Make more flash drives").
- **Please don't pass Vanguard on to other instructors, programs or schools.** Ask them to email support@winklers-llc.com instead, and they'll get their own clean copy — with no one else's data on it.

## 4. Your data stays with you
Vanguard stores everything on the computer (or USB drive) it runs on. **The Vanguard project does not receive, see or keep any student data.** The instructor's program and school decide how long records are kept and when they are deleted (Vanguard's End pilot and Delete profile tools help). See the **Privacy notice** for details.

## 5. Optional online AI
Online AI is **off** unless the person who manages the device turns it on with their own key from an outside AI provider. If they do, ordinary Ask questions go to that provider under **its** terms — read them first, especially about minors' data. Leave it off if you're unsure.

## 6. Use it responsibly
- Keep PINs private, keep the Class PIN to instructors, and keep the computer's own security on (screen lock, updates, disk encryption such as BitLocker).
- Use made-up profiles, not real Cadets, for demonstrations and practice.
- Don't use Vanguard to harass anyone, to collect information about people who haven't agreed to it, or for anything unlawful.
