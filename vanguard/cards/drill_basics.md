**Commands** (paras 3-3, 3-5): most drill commands have two parts — the **preparatory command** says what to do (*Forward*), the **command of execution** says when (**MARCH**). A few combine both, such as **FALL IN**, **AT EASE** and **REST**.

**Position of Attention** (paras 4-3 to 4-5) — on **FALL IN** or *Squad (Platoon)*, **ATTENTION**:
- Heels together on line, toes out equally to form a **45-degree angle**; weight on heels and balls of both feet.
- Legs straight without locking the knees; body erect, hips level, chest lifted, shoulders square.
- Head erect, face to the front, chin drawn in.
- Arms hang straight; fingers curled so the thumb tips touch the first joint of the forefingers; thumbs along the trouser seams.
- Silent, no movement. Enlisted Soldiers take this position when **addressing officers**.

**Rest positions at the halt** (para 4-6):
- **Parade Rest** (*Parade*, **REST** — only from Attention): left foot about **10 inches** to the left; hands at the small of the back centered on the belt, fingers extended and joined, **thumbs interlocked, right palm facing out**; head and eyes as at Attention; silent. Enlisted Soldiers take this position when **addressing NCOs**.
- **Stand at Ease** (*Stand at*, **EASE**): Parade Rest, but turn head and eyes toward the person in charge.
- **At Ease** (**AT EASE**): you may move, but stay standing and silent with your **right foot in place**.
- **Rest** (**REST**): you may move, **talk, or drink** — still standing with your right foot in place.
On the preparatory command for Attention, go straight to Parade Rest from any of these.

**Facing movements** (paras 4-7 to 4-9) — five, all from Attention: **Left FACE**, **Right FACE**, **Half Left FACE**, **Half Right FACE**, **About FACE**. Facing to the flank and to the rear are both **two-count** movements. The **Hand Salute** (*Present*, **ARMS**) is a one-count movement (para 4-10).

## Quiz
Q: What are the two parts of most drill commands? | A: The preparatory command and the command of execution
Q: In "Forward, MARCH", which part is the command of execution? | A: MARCH
Q: At the Position of Attention, what angle do your feet form? | A: 45 degrees
Q: At Parade Rest, how far do you move the left foot? | A: About 10 inches to the left
Q: How do you stand at Parade Rest? | A: From Attention, move the left foot about 10 inches to the left; hands at the small of the back centered on the belt, fingers extended and joined, thumbs interlocked, right palm out; head and eyes as at Attention; silent
Q: At Parade Rest, how are your hands placed? | A: At the small of the back centered on the belt, fingers extended and joined, thumbs interlocked, right palm facing out
Q: What position do enlisted Soldiers take when addressing an NCO? | A: Parade Rest
Q: What position do enlisted Soldiers take when addressing an officer? | A: Attention
Q: Which rest position allows talking? | A: Rest
Q: At At Ease, what must stay in place? | A: Your right foot — and you stay silent
Q: How many facing movements are there at the halt? | A: Five — Left, Right, Half Left, Half Right, and About Face
Q: How many counts is About Face? | A: Two
