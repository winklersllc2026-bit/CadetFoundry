**Names for the flag:**
- **National ensign** (or **ensign**): the national flag flown from Navy ships and flagstaffs ashore.
- **National colors**: the same flag mounted on a staff and carried on foot.
- **National standard**: the same flag mounted on a vehicle.

An NJROTC unit carries the **national colors** and its **organizational colors**. The organizational flag has the same design as the U.S. Naval Infantry flag, with "NJROTC" and the school's name in gold.

**Order of flags:**
1. National
2. State
3. Military, in order of service birthdays
4. Others, including NJROTC unit flags

The military birthdays used for this order are Army (14 June 1775), Marine Corps (10 November 1775), Navy (13 October 1775), Air Force (18 September 1947) and Coast Guard (4 August 1790). In wartime, when the Coast Guard operates under the Navy, it goes ahead of the Air Force.

**Rules for the colors:**
- The colors **never touch the deck**.
- The **national colors render no salute**. The organizational colors salute only during the national anthem, "To the Colors," "Retreat" or "Hail to the Chief," and for certain honors.
- The national colors are always carried when the organizational colors are, but may be carried alone.
- NJROTC color guards carry **drill rifles with no bayonets**.

**Morning and evening colors.** A detail of **three or more** Cadets raises the ensign at **0800** and lowers it at **sunset**.
- Mark the flag so the **blue field goes up first**. That keeps it from being raised upside down.
- For **half-mast**, hoist the ensign to the **peak** first, then lower it to half-mast.
- At evening colors, an ensign at half-mast is hoisted back to the peak, then lowered.
- Fold the ensign into a **tri-corner** shape, with the blue field outside.
