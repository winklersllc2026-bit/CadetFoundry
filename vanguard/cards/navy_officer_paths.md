**Navy ROTC (NROTC)** — a scholarship program for college students:
- Full tuition at a participating NROTC school, plus a monthly stipend.
- Three summer training cruises with surface ships, aviation squadrons and submarines.
- A traditional college experience while preparing to serve as a Naval Officer.

**The U.S. Naval Academy** — the undergraduate college of the naval service, in **Annapolis, Maryland**:
- Students are **midshipmen** on active duty in the U.S. Navy, for four years.
- They graduate with a **Bachelor of Science** and a commission as an **Ensign in the Navy** or a **Second Lieutenant in the Marine Corps**.
- Graduates serve at least **five years** in the Navy or Marine Corps.

Requirements and deadlines change — check with your instructor and the official program sites.
