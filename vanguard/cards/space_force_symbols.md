**The Delta.** The Space Force's main symbol is a stylized **delta**. Deltas have been used by the U.S. military in space missions since **1961**, under the Air Force and Air Force Space Command. The Delta Wing "evokes historic ties to the earliest days" of Air Force space work, and stands for **change and innovation**.

**Three official marks:**
- **Logo**: the main mark, a Delta with the service name.
- **Seal**: for official use only, such as policy letters, certificates and diplomas.
- **Emblem**: for things like plaques and office signs.

**What the seal shows:**
- **Dark blue and white**: the vastness of outer space.
- **Silver delta pointing upward**: the Space Force's central role defending the space domain. Its two shades of gray stand for **24/7 operations**.
- **Globe**: the Space Force's home on Earth and its support to joint warfighters.
- **Orbit ring**: defense from threats in space, and partnerships with allies and other agencies.
- **Polaris** (the North Star): security, and constant watchfulness in space.
- **Two star clusters**: the space assets the Space Force operates.
- **Three larger stars**: the jobs to **organize, train and equip**.
- **MMXIX**: Roman numerals for **2019**, the year the Space Force was created.

The President approved the seal on **January 15, 2020**.

**The song.** "**Semper Supra**" became the official Space Force song on **September 20, 2022**. It is named for the motto, Latin for "**Always Above**." Like "The Army Goes Rolling Along" and "The Marines' Hymn," it is part of the service's traditions.
