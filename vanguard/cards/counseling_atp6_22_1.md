**Three categories of developmental counseling** (para 2-4):
- **Performance counseling** — a review of duty performance over a set period; communicates standards and sets objectives. Required under the evaluation reporting systems (AR 623-3).
- **Professional growth counseling** — planning for individual and professional goals; builds an Individual Development Plan (DA Form 7906).
- **Event counseling** — about a specific event or situation: for example superior or substandard performance, reception and integration, or crisis counseling. It can come before events such as a promotion board, a school, or a deployment.

**The four-stage counseling process** (para 2-29):
1. **Identify the need** for counseling.
2. **Prepare** for counseling.
3. **Conduct** the counseling session.
4. **Follow up.**

**Three approaches** (paras 2-41 to 2-44):
- **Nondirective** — preferred for most sessions; the leader helps the subordinate develop their own solutions.
- **Directive** — best for on-the-spot corrections and simple problems; the leader directs the course of action.
- **Combined** — a mix: the subordinate plans and decides, and the leader may help analyze options or suggest solutions.

The counseling form is **DA Form 4856** (Developmental Counseling Form).
