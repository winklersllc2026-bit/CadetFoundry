A **credit report** is a summary of your personal credit history — the loans and credit cards you've had and how you've paid them.

**Who uses it:** credit bureaus share it with lenders and others. Businesses use it to decide **whether to lend you money and on what terms**.

**The three nationwide credit bureaus:** **Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.**

**Get it free:** federal law lets you get a free report from each bureau at least **once every 12 months**. The **only** website authorized for the free annual reports is **AnnualCreditReport.com** — be careful of look-alike sites.

**Check it:** once you have credit, look over your report for mistakes or accounts you don't recognize.
