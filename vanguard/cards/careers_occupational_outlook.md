The **Occupational Outlook Handbook (OOH)** is a free career guide from the **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics** (U.S. Department of Labor). It has more than 300 occupational profiles covering about 4 out of 5 jobs in the economy.

**Each profile tells you:**
- what workers do and their work environment
- the education, training and other qualifications needed
- pay
- the job outlook
- state and area data, similar occupations, and where to learn more

**How current:** job outlook and pay data are updated every year.

**Try it:** pick three careers that interest you, look each one up on bls.gov/ooh, and compare the education needed, pay and outlook. Your school counselor can help you plan the classes that lead there.
