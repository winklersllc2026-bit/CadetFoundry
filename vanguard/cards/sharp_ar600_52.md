**The goal** (para 1-6): a culture free of sexual harassment, sexual assault and retaliation. The program's two overarching responsibilities are **prevention and response**. **Prevention is the commander's responsibility.**

**Sexual harassment** (para 2-2): unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or deliberate or repeated offensive comments or gestures of a sexual nature — when it's made a condition of someone's job, pay or career; used for career decisions; or creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive working environment. It is punishable under the UCMJ (Arts. 92 and 134).

**Reporting sexual harassment** (paras 2-5 to 2-9):
- **Confidential report** to a SARC or Victim Advocate (VA): you get victim services and help, but the harassment is not investigated or resolved.
- **Anonymous complaint**: no personal information required.
- **Formal complaint**: filed on **DA Form 7746** with help from the SARC or VA; it is investigated.

**Reporting sexual assault — two options** (para 3-3):
- **Unrestricted**: triggers an **investigation** and **command notification**; you get healthcare, a SARC and a VA. It **cannot be changed** to a restricted report.
- **Restricted**: **no investigation**; the senior commander is told an assault was reported but **not who** reported it. You confidentially tell a **SARC, VA or healthcare personnel** and still get healthcare, a forensic exam (SAFE), counseling and a SARC and VA. You can **change it to unrestricted at any time**.
- Telling a friend or family member doesn't take away the restricted option. A report to a chaplain alone doesn't start SHARP services — that takes filing DD Form 2910 with a SARC or VA.

**Safe to Report** (para 3-9): covers minor collateral misconduct by a Soldier who is the victim of sexual assault — so fear of small infractions shouldn't stop a report.

**Help 24/7**: the **DoD Safe Helpline, 1-877-995-5247**, or your unit's SARC / SHARP VA.
