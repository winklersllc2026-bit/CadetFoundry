A **budget** is a plan for your money. Three steps:

1. **List your bills and other expenses** and how much each one costs.
2. **Write down how much money you make each month** (use your pay stubs). This is your **income**.
3. **Subtract your monthly expenses from your income.** The answer should be **more than zero**. If it's less than zero, you're spending more than you make — look for things to cut.

**Use it every month:**
- Plan your spending at the start of the month.
- Write down what you spend each day.
- At the end of the month, check whether you spent what you planned, and adjust next month.

**Pay yourself:** put leftover money into **savings** — you can even list savings as one of your budget expenses so it happens every month.

If your pay changes from month to month, add up last year's total income and divide by 12 to estimate a month.
