**Basics** (para 4-17)
- Every marching movement from the Halt starts at the **Position of Attention**.
- From the Halt, every step except Right Step begins with the **left foot**.
- A **step** is the distance from one heel to the other heel.
- Marching is in **Quick Time (120 steps per minute)**; the 30-inch step can also be done at **Double Time (180)**.

**The 30-inch step — *Forward*, MARCH** (para 4-18): on *Forward*, shift your weight to the right foot. On **MARCH**, step off 30 inches with the left foot. Arms swing naturally, about **9 inches to the front and 6 inches to the rear**, without bending the elbows.

**The Halt — *Squad (Platoon)*, HALT**: a **two-count** movement — take one more step, then bring the trail foot alongside the lead foot at Attention.

**Change Step** (para 4-19) — used when you're out of step with the formation.

**To the Rear, MARCH** (para 4-20) — turns the element **180 degrees**: one more step with the left foot, pivot right on the balls of both feet, step off in the new direction.

**Rest movements while marching** (para 4-21)
- **At Ease, MARCH** — no cadence required, but stay **silent** and keep your interval and distance.
- **Route Step, MARCH** — the same, but you may **talk and drink**.

**Other steps** (paras 4-22 to 4-26)
- **Half Step** — a **15-inch** step forward.
- **Mark Time** — march in place, raising each foot **2 inches**.
- **Right/Left Step** and **Backward** — 15-inch steps, given only from the Halt.
- **Double Time** — 30-inch steps at 180 per minute, forearms horizontal.

Distance between Soldiers while marching: **one arm's length plus 6 inches (about 40 inches)**.

## Quiz
Q: How long is a normal marching step? | A: 30 inches
Q: How long is a Half Step? | A: 15 inches
Q: With which foot do you step off from the Halt? | A: The left foot
Q: How far do your arms swing to the front and rear while marching? | A: About 9 inches to the front and 6 inches to the rear
Q: How many counts is the Halt? | A: Two
Q: What does Mark Time mean? | A: March in place
Q: Which marching rest movement lets you talk? | A: Route Step
Q: What command turns a marching unit 180 degrees? | A: Rear, MARCH
Q: What is the distance between Soldiers while marching? | A: One arm's length plus 6 inches — about 40 inches
Q: When do you use Change Step? | A: When you're out of step with the rest of the formation
