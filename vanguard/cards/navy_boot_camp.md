Navy boot camp is at **Recruit Training Command (RTC), Great Lakes, Illinois** — the Navy's only enlisted boot camp. Recruits are trained by **Recruit Division Commanders (RDCs)**. The Navy calls the process **"Sailorization."**

- **Processing days** — haircuts, uniform issue, medical and dental screening, an initial fitness event, and briefs on the UCMJ.
- **Week 1** — the **3rd Class Swim Qualification**: swim 50 yards, a 5-minute prone float and clothing inflation. Every Sailor who graduates must be a qualified swimmer. Core values, uniforms and grooming, watchstanding.
- **Week 2** — Navy ships and aircraft, naval history, the UCMJ, first aid, the first knowledge test.
- **Week 3** — basic seamanship and line handling on a training ship called the **USS Marlinspike**, and man-overboard drills.
- **Week 4** — **damage control**: firefighting, breathing devices and the confidence chamber; an RDC fitness assessment.
- **Week 5** — weapons handling, the small arms trainer and the Navy Handgun Qualification Course.
- **Week 6** — hands-on firefighting, personnel inspection, the second knowledge test.
- **Week 7** — final personnel inspection, firefighting assessment, Warrior Toughness.
- **Week 8** — the official fitness assessment and **Battle Stations 21**, a **12-hour** test aboard the training ship **USS Trayer** that pulls the whole course together.
- **Week 9** — graduation at the **Pass-in-Review** ceremony.

Information only — the schedule changes. Ask a recruiter for anything official.
