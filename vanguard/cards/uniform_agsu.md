Placement on the **Class A Army Green Service Uniform (AGSU) coat**, enlisted. Check the current DA PAM 670-1 and your leadership — guidance changes.

**Sleeves**
- **Rank** (sew-on): each sleeve, centered between the shoulder seam and the elbow. Men wear the large insignia, women the small. If the patch is in the way, rank goes 1/2 inch below it (fig 21-57).
- **Unit patch** (SSI): left sleeve, centered 1/2 inch below the top of the shoulder seam (fig 21-137).
- **Combat patch** (SSI-MOHC): right sleeve, centered 1/2 inch below the top of the right shoulder seam (fig 21-137).
- **Tabs**: up to two, centered 1/4 inch above the unit patch on the left sleeve (fig 22-69).
- **Service stripes**: outside bottom half of both sleeves, at a 30-degree angle; each extra stripe 1/8 inch above the last (fig 21-148).
- **Overseas service bars**: outside bottom half of the right sleeve; the first bar 4 inches above the bottom of the sleeve, each extra bar 1/16 inch above (fig 21-151).

**Collar and shoulders**
- **U.S. insignia**: right collar — bottom of the disk about 1 inch above the notch, centerline parallel to the inside edge of the lapel (fig 21-23).
- **Branch insignia**: left collar — bottom of the disk about 1 inch above the notch, centerline bisecting the notch and parallel to the inside edge of the lapel (fig 21-128). Basic trainees wear only the U.S. insignia until they are awarded their MOS.
- **DUI** (unit crest): centered on the shoulder loops, an equal distance from the outside shoulder seam to the outside edge of the button, base toward the seam (fig 21-138).

**Chest**
- **Ribbons**: centered 1/8 inch above the left breast pocket (fig 22-4).
- **Badges** (marksmanship, combat, special skill) on the left breast pocket flap: one badge centered with its top about 1/8 inch below the top of the pocket; two badges equally spaced with at least 1 inch between them; three equally spaced. No more than four badges in all (figs 22-26, 22-29, 22-32, 22-61).
- **Unit awards**: right side, bottom edge 1/8 inch above the right breast pocket flap (fig 22-17).
- **ID badges**: one per side. Men: centered on the pocket, between the bottom of the flap and the bottom of the pocket. Women: bottom of the badge level with the third buttonhole, centered on the pocket flap button (figs 22-87, 22-91).
- **No nameplate** on the Class A or Class B AGSU (para 14-5).

## Quiz
Q: Is a nameplate worn on the AGSU? | A: No — no nameplate on the Class A or Class B AGSU
Q: Where is rank worn on the AGSU coat (enlisted)? | A: On each sleeve, centered between the shoulder seam and the elbow
Q: Where does the unit patch (SSI) go on the AGSU coat? | A: Left sleeve, centered 1/2 inch below the top of the shoulder seam
Q: Where does the combat patch (SSI-MOHC) go on the AGSU coat? | A: Right sleeve, centered 1/2 inch below the top of the shoulder seam
Q: Where are ribbons worn on the AGSU coat? | A: Centered 1/8 inch above the left breast pocket
Q: Where are unit awards worn on the AGSU coat? | A: Right side, bottom edge 1/8 inch above the right breast pocket flap
Q: Which collar gets the U.S. insignia on the AGSU (enlisted)? | A: The right collar, bottom of the disk about 1 inch above the notch
Q: Which collar gets the branch insignia on the AGSU (enlisted)? | A: The left collar, bottom of the disk about 1 inch above the notch
Q: Where is the DUI worn on the AGSU coat? | A: Centered on the shoulder loops
Q: How many badges may be worn on the AGSU at one time? | A: Four, including marksmanship badges
Q: Where are overseas service bars worn on the AGSU (enlisted)? | A: Right sleeve, outside bottom half, first bar 4 inches above the bottom of the sleeve
