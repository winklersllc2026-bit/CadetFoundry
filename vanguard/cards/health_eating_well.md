The core message of the current Dietary Guidelines: **eat real food** — whole, nutrient-dense foods — and **cut way back on highly processed foods.**

**Build your meals around:**
- **Protein at every meal** — eggs, poultry, seafood, meat, beans, peas, lentils, nuts, seeds, soy. Bake, broil, roast or grill instead of deep-frying.
- **Vegetables and fruits** — colorful, whole, in their original form. Goals for a 2,000-calorie day: **3 servings of vegetables, 2 of fruit.** Frozen or canned without added sugar works too.
- **Dairy** — about **3 servings a day**, with no added sugars.
- **Whole grains** — **2–4 servings a day**, rich in fiber.
- **Water** — choose water (still or sparkling) and unsweetened drinks.

**Cut back on:**
- **Highly processed** salty or sweet foods — chips, cookies, candy.
- **Sugar-sweetened drinks — sodas, fruit drinks and energy drinks.**
- **Added sugar** — no more than **10 grams in one meal**. On labels, look for words with **"sugar" or "syrup"** or ending in **"-ose."**
- **Sodium** — under **2,300 mg a day** for ages 14 and up.

**For teens (ages 11–18):** adolescence is a **rapid growth period** with higher needs for **energy, protein, calcium and iron**. Calcium and vitamin D build peak bone strength. Eat foods like dairy, leafy greens and iron-rich foods, **significantly limit sugary and energy drinks**, and help with shopping and cooking so you learn to make healthy choices for life.
