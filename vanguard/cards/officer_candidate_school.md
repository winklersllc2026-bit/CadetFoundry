**GT score for OCS:** applicants need a **General Technical (GT) score of 110 or higher** (AR 350-51).

OCS has other requirements too — education, age, citizenship, a security clearance and a physical. Requirements can change, so confirm the current list with your **career counselor** or **S-1** before you apply.
