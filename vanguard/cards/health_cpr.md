**CPR** combines **chest compressions** and **rescue breaths** to keep blood flowing when someone's heart has stopped.

**The steps** (MedlinePlus — adults and teens):
1. **Check if they respond** — tap or gently shake them and shout, **"Are you OK?"**
2. **No response? Call 911** (or have someone call) and send someone for an **AED** if one is nearby. The 911 operator can coach you through CPR.
3. **Chest compressions** — put the heel of one hand **in the center of the chest, right between the nipples**, the other hand on top, fingers interlaced. Push down **about 2 inches** and let the chest **rise all the way back up** after each one.
   - **30 compressions** at **100 to 120 per minute.**
4. **Rescue breaths** — open the airway and give **2 breaths**.
5. **Repeat 30 and 2** until the person recovers or help arrives.

**AED (automated external defibrillator):** if one is available, **use it as soon as possible.**

**Learn it for real:** MedlinePlus notes CPR is best learned in an **accredited course** — ask your instructor or school nurse about classes. This card is a study guide, not a substitute for training.
