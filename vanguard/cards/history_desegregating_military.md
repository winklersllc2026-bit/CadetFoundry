**Black Americans served in the Union forces** — by the end of the Civil War, almost **200,000** Black soldiers and sailors had served.

**Executive Order 9981 — July 26, 1948**
- Signed by **President Harry S. Truman**.
- Declared the policy of "**equality of treatment and opportunity for all persons in the armed services** without regard to race, color, religion or national origin."
- **Banned segregation** in the Armed Forces and called for full integration of all branches.
- Created an **advisory committee** to recommend changes to military rules.

**How it took hold:** some in the military resisted for a while, but the order's effects took hold — **by the end of the Korean conflict, almost all of the military was integrated.**

**Today:** the Army Value of **Respect** — treat people as they should be treated — applies to every Soldier and every Cadet.
