**FAFSA** stands for the **Free Application for Federal Student Aid**. It is free to fill out.

Colleges, career schools, the federal government and many states use it to decide what financial aid you can get — for example **grants** (money you don't pay back), **work-study** jobs, and **student loans**.

**Good to know:**
- Fill it out for each school year you want aid.
- Deadlines differ by college and by state, and some aid is limited — earlier is better.
- Your school counselor can help, and studentaid.gov has the official instructions and dates.

Never pay a website to file the FAFSA for you — the official form at studentaid.gov is free.
