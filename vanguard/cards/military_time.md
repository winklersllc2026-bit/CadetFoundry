The Army uses a 24-hour clock. Midnight is 0000, noon is 1200.
For times after noon, add 12 to the hour: 1:00 p.m. = 1300, 6:30 p.m. = 1830, 11:59 p.m. = 2359.
Say it as "thirteen hundred", "eighteen thirty". Morning times keep the leading zero: 7:00 a.m. = 0700 ("zero seven hundred").

## Quiz
Q: What is 3:00 p.m. in military time? | A: 1500
Q: What is 1945 in regular time? | A: 7:45 p.m.
Q: What is midnight in military time? | A: 0000
Q: What is 9:15 a.m. in military time? | A: 0915
Q: How do you say 0600 out loud? | A: Zero six hundred
