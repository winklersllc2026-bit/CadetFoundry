The Marine Corps has two fitness tests a new Marine must meet:

- **Initial Strength Test (IST)** — given at recruit training. It confirms a recruit can safely begin training. Events: **pull-ups or push-ups, a plank, and a timed 1.5-mile run.**
- **Physical Fitness Test (PFT)** — the formal, scored test. Recruits take it several times in recruit training, and Marines take it **every year** after. Events: **pull-ups or push-ups, a plank, and a timed 3-mile run.** Passing the PFT is required to graduate boot camp.

Both measure **muscular endurance, muscular strength and cardiovascular endurance**. The Marine Corps is blunt about it: "effort is not enough … repetitions must meet proper form." Getting in shape before recruit training is the recruit's own responsibility.

**For Cadets:** the same idea works for your own fitness goals — learn the standard, practice it with correct form, and track your progress in 💪 Train.
