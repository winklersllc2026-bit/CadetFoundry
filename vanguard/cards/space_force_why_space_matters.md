**Space is part of everyday life.** SFDD-1 says **at least half** of America's critical infrastructure depends on space: GPS, communications, timing and weather. The joint force is built on the assumption that military spacepower is its **backbone**.

**Space is a warfighting domain.** The Space Force does not only provide support. Guardians must be ready to **contest and control** space so that U.S. forces keep their freedom of action.

**Space superiority** is the degree of control that lets friendly forces operate at a time and place of their choosing **without prohibitive interference**, while **denying the same** to an adversary.
- **Space threats** are enemy space systems that help them attack U.S. forces.
- **Counterspace threats** are enemy systems that put U.S. space systems at risk.

**Competitive Endurance** is the Space Force's approach to gaining space superiority while keeping space safe and usable for the long term. It has three tenets:
1. **Avoid operational surprise**: watch the domain closely enough to see changes coming.
2. **Deny first-mover advantage**: be resilient enough that striking first in space wouldn't pay off for an enemy.
3. **Conduct responsible counterspace operations**: protect the nation without creating long-lived, hazardous debris.

**Statutory roles.** Title 10 of U.S. law says the Space Force is organized, trained and equipped to:
- provide freedom of operation in, from and to space;
- conduct space operations;
- protect U.S. interests in space.
