**The NJROTC patch** shows a **ship, an anchor and an eagle**. It is worn on the **left sleeve**, with the top edge 1 inch below the shoulder seam. It goes on the khaki shirt, the Service Dress Blue coat and the black relaxed fit jacket. It is not worn on raincoats or peacoats.

**Collar devices:**
- The **JROTC device** goes on the **left** collar.
- **Rank or rate** insignia goes on the **right** collar.

**Caps:**
- A **fouled anchor** is worn on the cap headband, and on the **left** side of the garrison cap.
- Cadet officers and chiefs wear rank on the **right** side of the garrison cap.

**Service stars** are gold, five-pointed stars that show how many NJROTC years or courses you have completed. They are worn ¼ inch above the left pocket or ribbons, two points down.

**Honor roll.** A Cadet on the school honor roll wears a **service star above the JROTC collar device**, or two stars for the principal's honor roll. It comes off if you're no longer on the current honor roll.

**Ribbons and medals:**
- Ribbons are worn in rows of three, ¼ inch above the left pocket, with the most senior ribbon on the top row, inboard.
- **NJROTC ribbons come first**. Awards from other organizations are worn after them.
- **Medals** hang below the ribbons in this order: NJROTC **academic** competition, then NJROTC **marksmanship** competition, then all others.

**Drone Wings** are earned by passing the FAA **Part 107** drone pilot test. They are worn ¼ inch above the ribbons and below the service stars.
