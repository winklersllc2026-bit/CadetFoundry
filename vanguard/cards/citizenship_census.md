**The Constitution requires a count of everyone in the country every 10 years.** Article I, Section 2: the "actual Enumeration shall be made ... within every subsequent **Term of ten Years**." That's the **decennial census**.

**Why the framers wanted it:** they chose **population** as the basis for sharing political power.

**What the census decides** (Census Bureau):
- **Apportionment** — dividing the **435 seats in the House of Representatives** among the states. States that grow can gain seats; states that shrink can lose them.
- **Districts** — the results are used to **redraw electoral districts**, usually by state legislatures or redistricting commissions.
- **Money** — the results help decide how **trillions of dollars in federal funding** are distributed over the next ten years.

**Why it matters to you:** an accurate count means fair representation and a fair share of funding for your town. When the census comes, help your family make sure everyone in your home is counted.
