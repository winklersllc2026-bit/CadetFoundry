**The Army's birthday is 14 June 1775** — the U.S. Army was founded **by action of the Continental Congress** on that date. It is older than the country itself (the Declaration of Independence came in 1776). June 14 is also **Flag Day**.

**The U.S. Army flag** (para I-10)
- **White silk**, with the **seal of the Department of the Army** in **ultramarine blue**.
- A **scarlet scroll** reading **"United States Army"** in white, and the blue numerals **"1775"** for the year the Army was founded.
- **Yellow fringe** on three sides.
- It was first unfurled on **Flag Day, 14 June 1956** — the Army's **181st** anniversary — at **Independence Hall in Philadelphia**.
- The seal at its center comes from the **War Office seal**, authorized by the Continental Congress on **8 May 1779**.
- The flag carries **streamers for every Army campaign** since 1775.

**The Army field flag** (para I-11) — authorized in 1962 for units not issued the Army flag: an ultramarine blue background with a white seal, and no streamers.

**Who carries the Colors?** Until 1813, regimental Colors were carried by **ensigns**, the lowest-ranking officers. Since then the job has gone to **Color sergeants** — **NCOs carry them today** (para I-9).
