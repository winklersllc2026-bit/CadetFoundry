Marine leadership builds on the core values with **14 leadership traits**, remembered as **JJ DID TIE BUCKLE**:

- **J**ustice — fairness and impartiality
- **J**udgment — making sound decisions
- **D**ependability — can be counted on to carry out a task
- **I**nitiative — adapting when the situation changes
- **D**ecisiveness — giving orders clearly, forcefully and promptly
- **T**act — treating everyone with respect and courtesy
- **I**ntegrity — being truthful and honest
- **E**nthusiasm — exuberance in doing your duty
- **B**earing — looking, talking and acting like a leader
- **U**nselfishness — taking care of your Marines first
- **C**ourage — doing what is right despite danger or criticism
- **K**nowledge — technically and tactically proficient
- **L**oyalty — faithful to Country, Corps, unit, seniors, peers and subordinates
- **E**ndurance — the physical and mental stamina to withstand pain, fatigue, stress and hardship

**The 11 leadership principles:**
1. Be technically and tactically proficient.
2. Know yourself and seek self-improvement.
3. Know your Marines and look out for their welfare.
4. Keep your Marines informed.
5. Set the example.
6. Ensure the task is understood, supervised, and accomplished.
7. Train your Marines as a team.
8. Make sound and timely decisions.
9. Develop a sense of responsibility among your subordinates.
10. Employ your unit in accordance with its capabilities.
11. Seek responsibility and take responsibility for your actions.

**A tradition that shows it:** when hot chow is served in the field, junior Marines eat first, then NCOs, then staff NCOs, and **officers eat last**.
