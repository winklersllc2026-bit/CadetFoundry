**The flag detail** is normally an **NCOIC** (the NCO in charge), **two halyard pullers**, and **two to eight flag handlers**. The handlers make sure the flag is folded correctly and **never touches the ground**. As a guide: **2 handlers** for the storm (small) flag, **6** for the post (medium) flag, **8** for the garrison (large) flag.

**Raising the flag (Reveille)** (paras I-1, I-3)
- The flag is raised at the **first note of Reveille**, all the way to the top.
- **Half-staff:** hoist it **all the way to the top first**, then lower it to half-staff.
- The NCOIC and handlers salute as the flag goes up.

**Lowering the flag (Retreat)** (paras I-2, I-4)
- After **Retreat** plays, the flag is lowered **slowly and with dignity** during **"To the Color"** (or the national anthem).
- A half-staffed flag is **raised to the top before it is lowered**.

**Folding** (para I-5; AR 600-25)
- Fold it **lengthwise twice**, with the edge overlapping so **no red shows**.
- Fold a **4-inch cuff**, then fold the flag in **triangles** into the shape of a **cocked hat**.
- Keep the edges straight; no part of the flag touches the ground.
- The NCOIC inspects it and carries it against the chest, **point up**.

Once folded, the flag is treated like a **cased Color**: with utmost dignity and respect, but people meeting the detail don't salute it.

**The flag represents the living country and is considered a living thing** (para I-6).

## Quiz
Q: Who is in a flag detail? | A: An NCOIC, two halyard pullers, and two to eight flag handlers
Q: What is the main job of the flag handlers? | A: To fold the flag correctly and keep it from touching the ground
Q: When is the flag raised in the morning? | A: At the first note of Reveille
Q: How do you raise a flag to half-staff? | A: Hoist it all the way to the top first, then lower it to half-staff
Q: What do you do before lowering a flag that is at half-staff? | A: Raise it all the way to the top first
Q: During which bugle call is the flag lowered? | A: To the Color, after Retreat
Q: What shape is a properly folded flag? | A: A triangle — the shape of a cocked hat
Q: How many flag handlers are used for a garrison flag? | A: Eight
Q: Once the flag is folded, how is it treated? | A: Like a cased Color — with dignity and respect, but not saluted
