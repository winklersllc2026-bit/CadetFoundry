**The standard** (para 4-16): treat all people with **dignity and respect**, in person and online. **Hazing, bullying and discriminatory harassment are prohibited** — violators may be punished under the UCMJ or face administrative action.

**Discrimination** (para 6-2): when someone is harassed, intimidated, insulted, humiliated or treated less favorably **because of race, color, sex, national origin, religion, or sexual orientation** — including disparaging terms that contribute to a hostile work environment.
- The **chain of command** has primary responsibility for identifying and correcting discriminatory harassment.
- The policies apply **on and off post, on duty and off duty**, at work, in living areas and in recreation.
- No one may **retaliate** against a Soldier who files a complaint, **knowingly make a false accusation**, or — as a leader — **condone or ignore** discrimination or harassment.

**Hazing** (para 4-16a): conduct without proper military authority that physically or psychologically injures — or risks injuring — a Soldier as a condition of joining, being accepted, or changing status in a group. It can happen in person, by phone or on social media.

**Complaints** (para 6-6): try to resolve concerns at the **lowest possible level** first.
- **Informal**: not filed in writing; can be resolved by talking to the person, a peer, someone in the chain of command or NCO channel, or the MEO professional. When practical, resolved **within 60 calendar days**.
- **Formal**: filed in writing on **DA Form 7279**, at the lowest echelon of command — battalion or equivalent, **but no lower** — and not with the subject's own immediate company or battalion commander.
- **Anonymous** complaints can be handled as informal or formal.
- Crimes (UCMJ violations) are reported to law enforcement.

**Sexual assault**: the **DoD Safe Helpline, 1-877-995-5247**, or your unit's SHARP professional.
