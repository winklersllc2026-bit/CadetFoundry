**Space is crowded.** SFDD-1 describes space as harsh, highly radiated and **"congested by environmental and human-made debris."** Every active satellite shares orbit with dead satellites, rocket parts and fragments.

**Space domain awareness (SDA)** is one of the Space Force's four enterprise functions. It means:
- **detecting** objects and activity in space;
- **characterizing** them (what is it?);
- **attributing** them (whose is it?);
- **predicting** what they'll do next;
- **targeting**, when needed;

all to support good decisions.

**How it's done.** The Space Force says a "global network of space surveillance sensors" tracks the location of satellites and space debris. The information is shared with "the nation and the world."

**Why it matters for warfighting.** The first tenet of **Competitive Endurance** is **"Avoid operational surprise."** Guardians must spot changes in space early, using intelligence, environmental monitoring and reports from partners.

**Keeping space usable.** Another tenet is **responsible counterspace operations**. The Space Force aims to protect the nation **without creating long-lived, hazardous debris**. Long-lived debris is a hazard to every satellite in orbit.

**Working with others.** The **Office of Space Commerce** handles space traffic management with the Space Force, and allies share data. The goal is a space domain that is "safe, secure, stable, and sustainable."
