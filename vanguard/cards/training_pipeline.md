- **Basic Combat Training (BCT)** — **10 weeks in four phases** (goarmy.com):
  - **Yellow — weeks 1–2:** adapt to Army life; discipline, teamwork, the Army Values; begin physical and tactical training; obstacle courses.
  - **Red — weeks 3–4:** weapons and equipment familiarization, hand-to-hand combat, life-saving skills; first field exercise, **The Hammer**.
  - **White — weeks 5–7:** basic rifle marksmanship and target engagement, small-team skills; a two-day, two-night field exercise, **The Anvil**.
  - **Blue — weeks 8–10:** advanced weapons such as machine guns and grenades; a multiple-day field exercise, **The Forge** — your final test.
- **Advanced Individual Training (AIT)** — after BCT, you learn your job (your **MOS**, Military Occupational Specialty). Length depends on the job: a few weeks to over a year.
- **One Station Unit Training (OSUT)** — for some jobs (for example infantry), BCT and AIT are combined at one location with the same cadre.
- After training you go to your first unit (your "duty station").

Your **battle buddy** is the Soldier you are paired with — you look out for each other and never go anywhere alone in training.
