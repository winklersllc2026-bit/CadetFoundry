Terms you'll hear from day one (FM 7-22 glossary):
- **IMT** — initial military training. It includes the **FSP**, **BCT**, **AIT** and **OSUT** (FM 7-22, para 2-2).
- **FSP** — Future Soldier Program: the time before you ship, guided by your recruiter.
- **BCT** — basic combat training.
- **AIT** — **advanced individual training**. AIT focuses on technical, **MOS**-oriented subjects — the skills of your Army job — and physical training continues (FM 7-22, para 2-10).
- **OSUT** — one station unit training (basic and job training together, at one place).
- **MOS** — military occupational specialty: your Army job.
- **FUA** — first unit of assignment: the unit you join after training.
- **NCO** — noncommissioned officer. **SGT** — sergeant. **SFC** — sergeant first class.
- **H2F** — holistic health and fitness, the Army's fitness system.
- **AFT** — the **Army Fitness Test**. It replaced the Army Combat Fitness Test (**ACFT**) (ATP 7-22.02).
- **ABCP** — Army Body Composition Program.
- **ACU** — Army combat uniform.
- **FTU** — fitness training unit. **FTX** — field training exercise.
- **AR** — Army regulation. **FM** — field manual. **ATP** — Army techniques publication. **DA Pam** — Department of the Army pamphlet.
- **TRADOC** — United States Training and Doctrine Command.
