- **Your pay**: check your **LES** (Leave and Earnings Statement) every month in myPay. Report mistakes to your finance office quickly.
- **Thrift Savings Plan (TSP)**: the military's retirement savings plan. Under the **Blended Retirement System**, the government puts in an automatic 1% of your basic pay and matches what you contribute, up to 5% total. **Contribute at least 5% so you don't leave matching money on the table.** Ask finance when your matching starts.
- **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)**: interest on debts you had *before* active duty can be capped at **6%** — you have to request it from the lender in writing with your orders.
- **Military Lending Act (MLA)**: most consumer loans to active-duty members are capped at **36%** total annual rate (MAPR). Payday lenders, car dealers near bases and "military loans" with high fees are the classic traps.
- **Before a big purchase** (car, loan, ring), talk to a **Personal Financial Counselor** — free through Army Community Service or Military OneSource (800-342-9647).
- Build an emergency fund, even a small one. Avoid lending money to people in your unit.

This is general education, not financial advice. Rules and percentages can change — confirm with your finance office.
