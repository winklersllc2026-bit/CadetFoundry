**At the top.** The **Chief of Space Operations (CSO)** leads the Space Force. The CSO answers to the Secretary of the Air Force and is a member of the **Joint Chiefs of Staff**. The **Vice Chief of Space Operations** and the **Chief Master Sergeant of the Space Force** support the CSO.

**Department of the Air Force.** The Space Force and the Air Force are two separate Armed Services inside one department, the **Department of the Air Force**. Airmen provide many services to Guardians, including personnel, medical, legal, finance and base support.

**From big to small:**
- **Field commands (FLDCOMs)** report to the CSO. They train, educate, equip and prepare Guardians.
- **Deltas** sit under the field commands and run the day-to-day work. There are several kinds: Space Deltas (training, doctrine, tests, exercises), System Deltas (acquisition), Mission Deltas, Space Launch Deltas (spaceports and ranges) and Space Base Deltas (running installations).
- **Squadrons** do most of the mission work, under their parent Delta.
- **Detachments** are smaller units, often located away from the main unit, with their own commander.

**Getting ready: SPAFORGEN.** Space Force Generation has three phases:
1. **Prepare**: rest, leave, resupply, schooling and qualification training.
2. **Ready**: exercises and squadron validations.
3. **Commit**: fully ready and assigned to a combatant command for a real mission.

**One force, full- or part-time.** The Space Force does away with the separate active-duty and Reserve structure. Guardians can serve full-time or part-time within one force.
