The Navy's **Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA)** has two parts:

- **Body composition** — Sailors must be within approved body composition standards.
- **Physical Readiness Test (PRT)** — push-ups, a **timed plank**, and a **1.5-mile run**.

At boot camp, recruits get a check in **week 4** (an RDC assessment of planks, push-ups and the 1.5-mile run) and take the **official PFA in week 8**. Recruits must pass it to graduate.

Sailors are tested in **PFA cycles** during the year. The rules are in **OPNAVINST 6110.1L** (Change 1, approved July 2026) and the Navy's Physical Readiness Program guides, and they change over time. Check the official Physical Readiness page for current standards.
