**Terrain features** (on a map, shown by contour lines):
- Five major: **Hill, Ridge, Valley, Saddle, Depression**
- Three minor: **Draw, Spur, Cliff**
- Two supplementary: **Cut, Fill**

**Reading a grid**: read **right, then up**. Find the vertical grid line to the left of the point (the easting), then the horizontal line below it (the northing). More digits = more precise: a 6-digit grid is accurate to 100 meters, an 8-digit grid to 10 meters.

**Pace count**: the number of steps it takes you to walk 100 meters. Know yours and keep track of distance with it (beads or knots help).

**Map colors**: black = man-made features, blue = water, green = vegetation, brown = contour lines and terrain.
