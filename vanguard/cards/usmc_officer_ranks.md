Marine Corps commissioned officers, O-1 to O-10:

- **O-1** Second Lieutenant (2ndLt)
- **O-2** First Lieutenant (1stLt)
- **O-3** Captain (Capt)
- **O-4** Major (Maj)
- **O-5** Lieutenant Colonel (LtCol)
- **O-6** Colonel (Col)
- **O-7** Brigadier General (BGen)
- **O-8** Major General (MajGen)
- **O-9** Lieutenant General (LtGen)
- **O-10** General (Gen)

**Warrant officers** (W-1 to W-5) are technical experts: Warrant Officer 1, then Chief Warrant Officer 2 through 5.

The titles match the Army and Air Force, but each service writes the abbreviations its own way — for example, a Marine second lieutenant is **2ndLt**, an Army one is **2LT**, and an Air Force one is **2d Lt**.
