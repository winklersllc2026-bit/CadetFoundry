The Space Force's four values, as the Guardian Spirit handbook puts them:

- **Character… above all**
- **Connection… toward unity**
- **Commitment… to mastery**
- **Courage… to be bold**

**Character comes first** because it "lays the foundation for trust, teamwork, and good order and discipline." Without it, the other three values fall apart. Guardians embrace "integrity, grit, honesty, authenticity, and trustworthiness" and stay accountable for their decisions, actions and inactions.

**Connection:** "The difference between a group of individuals existing in a shared place and a team is the connection the individuals have to one another." The handbook quotes a proverb: "If you want to go fast, go alone; if you want to go far, go together."

**Commitment to mastery:** a lifelong pursuit of mastery "within ourselves, our profession, and our domain … a lifelong journey, not a destination."

**Courage to be bold:** "Guardians do what needs to be done and say what needs to be said because it is right."

**The Guardian Commitment** turns the values into "I will" statements for every team member, such as:
- "I will act with integrity, honesty, candor, and dedication to the team."
- "I will be accountable to you and the team for my decisions and actions."
- "I will uphold the highest standards and live our values."

Team leaders add **fairness** and **transparency**: they "explain 'the why'" instead of simply giving orders.
