The Space Force has **three core functions**: the broad, lasting jobs it organizes, trains and equips Guardians to do in, from and to space.

**1. Space control.** Contest and control the space domain. The goal is **space superiority**: being able to operate when and where you choose without prohibitive interference, while denying the same to an adversary. Its three mission areas are:
- **Orbital warfare**
- **Electromagnetic warfare**
- **Cyberspace warfare**

**2. Global mission operations.** Deliver space effects to military operations, so the U.S. can "out-see, out-shoot, out-maneuver, and out-communicate any adversary." Its five mission areas are:
- **Satellite communications**
- **Navigation warfare**, which assures or denies positioning, navigation and timing (GPS-type) data
- **Missile warning and tracking**: detect launches, track them and warn
- **Space-based sensing and targeting**
- **Theater electromagnetic warfare**

**3. Space access.** Move equipment and people into space, back to Earth and through space. It "sets the space theater." Its mission areas are:
- **Satellite control**: telemetry, tracking and commanding
- **Spacelift**: launching payloads
- **Launch range control**: a safe, secure range for launches

**Four enterprise functions** support all three: **intelligence**, **cyberspace operations**, **command and control**, and **space domain awareness**. Space domain awareness means detecting, characterizing, attributing and predicting activity in space.
