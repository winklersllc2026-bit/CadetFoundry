**NJROTC mission:** "to instill in students in United States secondary educational institutions the values of citizenship, service to the United States, personal responsibility, and a sense of accomplishment."

- Congress authorized Navy JROTC in **1964**.
- Instructors are **retired Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard** officers and enlisted members.
- The curriculum covers citizenship and leadership, **maritime heritage**, the significance of **sea power**, and naval topics such as naval operations, **seamanship, navigation and meteorology**.
- Outside class: community service, drill competitions, field meets, flights, visits to naval activities and marksmanship.

Being in NJROTC doesn't mean you'll join the Navy — or join the military at all. It's a citizenship and leadership program.
