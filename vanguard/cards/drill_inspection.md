**The formation:** a platoon is inspected in ranks in **line formation** (para 7-56). Squads are usually inspected by their **squad leaders**, but the platoon sergeant or platoon leader may do it (para 7-61).

**Getting ready** (paras 7-59, 7-60)
1. ***Count*, OFF** — everyone counts off from right to left.
2. ***Open Ranks*, MARCH** — the **first rank takes two steps forward**, the **second rank one step forward**, the **third rank stands fast**, and the **fourth rank takes two steps backward**. This opens space for the inspector to walk between the ranks.

**How the inspector moves** (paras 7-62 to 7-66)
- On **INSPECT YOUR SQUADS**, each squad leader marches to a point **15 inches in front of** the first Cadet. **Salutes are not exchanged.**
- The inspector stays at a modified Attention, **moving only head and eyes** — inspecting from the center, then a step to the left, then a step to the right.
- After the last Cadet, the inspector marches around **behind** the squad and inspects from the rear.
- An inspected squad goes to **At Ease** once its leader is back at their post.

**Finishing** (para 7-68): the platoon is called to Attention, then ***Close Ranks*, MARCH** — the first rank takes **four steps back**, the second **two back**, the third stands fast, the fourth takes **one step forward**.

**In-quarters (stand-by) inspection** (paras 7-79, 7-80): the squad leader reports, **"Sergeant, the platoon is prepared for inspection."** Each person comes to **Attention** as the inspector approaches and returns to **At Ease** after. **CARRY ON** is called as the inspector leaves.

**Being inspected — what you control:** a clean, correct uniform, a fresh haircut, polished shoes, and standing still at Attention with your eyes forward.

Q: In what formation is a platoon inspected in ranks? | A: Line formation
Q: What command opens space between the ranks for an inspection? | A: Open Ranks, MARCH
Q: On Open Ranks, what does the first rank do? | A: Takes two steps forward
Q: On Open Ranks, what does the third rank do? | A: Stands fast
Q: Are salutes exchanged when squad leaders inspect their squads? | A: No
Q: How far in front of a Cadet does the inspector stop? | A: 15 inches
Q: After inspecting the front of the squad, where does the inspector go? | A: Around behind the squad to inspect from the rear
Q: What command closes the ranks after an inspection? | A: Close Ranks, MARCH
