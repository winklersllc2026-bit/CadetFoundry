**The Army's definition:** Leadership is the activity of influencing people by providing **purpose, direction, and motivation** to accomplish the mission and improve the organization.

- **Influencing** — persuading people to do what is necessary.
- **Purpose** — gives people a reason to achieve the outcome. Good leaders explain *why*.
- **Direction** — telling others what to do: set priorities, assign responsibility, supervise, and make sure the work meets the standard.
- **Motivation** — the will and initiative to do what is necessary to accomplish the mission.

The strongest way to lead is by **personal example** — a leader shares the same hardships as the team.

**The leadership requirements model:**
- **Attributes (who a leader is — BE and KNOW):** Character, Presence, Intellect.
- **Competencies (what a leader does — DO):** Leads, Develops, Achieves.
- Competencies can be trained; attributes are shaped through experience.

**Three levels of leadership:** direct, organizational, strategic.

You don't need a rank to lead — a squad leader, team captain or older sibling uses the same three tools.
