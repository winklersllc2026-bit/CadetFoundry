Air Force Basic Military Training is a **7.5-week** transformation. Each trainee is assigned to a squadron with a **Military Training Instructor (MTI)**.

- **Week 0** — arrival: clothing, running shoe and equipment issue; learning the dorm and the dining hall.
- **Week 1** — rank and military etiquette, the oath and the Airman's Creed, the basics of marching.
- **Week 2** — squadron leaders begin to form; harder drill; Air Force history and heritage; the Wingman's real responsibilities; the issued rifle.
- **Week 3** — weapons and tactical movement; character and leadership; fitness progress checks.
- **Week 4** — career progression and quality of life; open-ranks dress and appearance inspection; OPSEC and sexual assault prevention and reporting.
- **Week 5** — handling stress, the laws of armed conflict, live fire at the range, practice for graduation.
- **Week 6 — Pacer Forge**, the most intense week: a simulated deployment with tactical combat casualty care, chemical-biological defense and combat scenarios. MTIs step back so trainees lead.
- **Week 7** — the **Airman's Run** and **graduation**, where trainees take the oath and read the Airman's Creed together as Airmen.

After BMT, Airmen keep their fitness up with fitness tests twice a year.
