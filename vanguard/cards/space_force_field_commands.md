**A lean structure with four levels:**
1. **Headquarters**, at the Pentagon
2. **Field commands**
3. **Deltas**
4. **Squadrons**

To stay lean, the Space Force cut out **two levels of command** that the Air Force has.

**The three field commands:**

**Space Systems Command (SSC)** builds the gear. It acquires, develops and delivers space capabilities. Its mission is "to develop and field dominant space capabilities."

**Space Training and Readiness Command (STARCOM)** trains the people. It prepares Guardians through education and training, writes space warfighting **doctrine**, and tests Space Force capabilities.
- Activated **August 23, 2021**, as the third field command.
- Headquarters at **Patrick Space Force Base, Florida**.
- It wrote **SFDD-1**, the Space Force's main doctrine document.

**Combat Forces Command (CFC)** delivers the fighting units. It generates and presents combat-ready intelligence, cyber, space and combat support forces to combatant commanders.
- It was **Space Operations Command (SpOC)** until **November 3, 2025**.
- About **12,000** Guardians, Airmen and civilians in 11 Deltas and 82 squadrons.
- Its vision: "**Always Ready, Always Innovative, Always Above.**"

**How they fit together:** SSC builds the systems, STARCOM trains Guardians to use them, and CFC turns trained units into combat-ready forces for the joint fight.
