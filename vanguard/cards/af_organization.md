From largest to smallest, the Air Force's field units are:

1. **Major commands** (MAJCOMs) — organized by function in the United States and by geography overseas
2. **Numbered air forces**
3. **Wings**
4. **Groups**
5. **Squadrons**
6. **Flights**

The **wing** is "the basic unit for generating and employing combat capability" — the Air Force's prime war-fighting unit.

Besides active duty, the Air Force has two Reserve components: the **Air Force Reserve** and the **Air National Guard**.
