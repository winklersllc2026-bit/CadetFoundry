**Motto: "Semper Supra"** is Latin for **"Always Above."** SFDD-1 calls securing the ultimate high ground the Guardians' "duty and promise to the American people."

**The Guardian Spirit** is described in three parts:
- **Principled Public Servant**: lives the values of character, connection, commitment and courage. Selfless public service is where the American people's trust comes from.
- **Space-minded Warfighter**: committed to defending the nation, an expert in space operations, and part of the joint team.
- **Bold and Collaborative Problem Solver**: debates new ideas, challenges the status quo, and has the courage to "experiment, fail, learn, adapt, and innovate." Comfortable empowering subordinates to act.

**Every Guardian is a warfighter.** Each member has a role:
- **Officers** are "principally leaders and planners."
- **Enlisted** Guardians are "the Service's warfighters": frontline operators with technical expertise. They are the main source of instructors and innovators. Senior enlisted Guardians advise leaders at every level.
- **Civilian** Guardians bring specialization, stability and continuity.

**Mission command** is how Guardians lead. The commander gives clear intent: the goal, the reason, and any limits. Then trust and **disciplined initiative** let the team decide how to reach that goal, even when plans change.
