**Federalism** means power is **shared** between the **national (federal) government** and the **state governments**. Both make laws that affect you.

**The 10th Amendment** — "The powers not delegated to the United States by the Constitution, nor prohibited by it to the States, are **reserved to the States respectively, or to the people**." Powers the Constitution doesn't give the federal government belong to the states or the people.

**The Supremacy Clause** (Article VI) — the Constitution, federal laws made under it, and treaties are "**the supreme Law of the Land**." If a state law conflicts with them, the federal law wins, and state judges are bound by it.

**Full Faith and Credit** (Article IV) — each state must respect the public acts, records and court decisions of every other state.

**A republican form of government** (Article IV) — the United States **guarantees every state a republican form of government** (government by elected representatives) and protects each state against invasion.

**Your state and local governments** make many of the laws and decisions closest to your daily life. USA.gov lists how to contact your state, its governor and major state agencies, and your local government.
