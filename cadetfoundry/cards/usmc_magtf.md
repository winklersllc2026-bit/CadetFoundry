The **Marine Air-Ground Task Force (MAGTF)** is the Marine Corps' principal warfighting organization. It puts ground, air and logistics Marines under **one command**.

Every MAGTF has four elements:
- **Command Element (CE)** — the headquarters: communications, intelligence and leadership.
- **Ground Combat Element (GCE)** — infantry, supported by armored vehicles, artillery, reconnaissance and engineers.
- **Aviation Combat Element (ACE)** — jets, helicopters, tiltrotor aircraft and drones, and the crews that keep them flying.
- **Logistics Combat Element (LCE)** — vehicles, power, water, transportation, medical and food.

MAGTFs come in different sizes: the **Marine Expeditionary Unit (MEU)**, **Marine Expeditionary Brigade (MEB)** and **Marine Expeditionary Force (MEF)**. The MEU is the smallest and fastest — about **2,200 Marines** commanded by a colonel, carried aboard **three Navy amphibious ships**, and supplied for **15 days**.
