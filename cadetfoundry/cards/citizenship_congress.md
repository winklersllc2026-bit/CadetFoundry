**Congress** has two chambers: the **House of Representatives** and the **Senate**.

**The House of Representatives** (house.gov)
- **435** voting members.
- The number of representatives per state is **based on population**.
- Each representative serves a **two-year term** and represents a **congressional district**.
- To serve: at least **25 years old**, a U.S. citizen for at least **7 years**, and an inhabitant of the state.
- Its leader is the **Speaker of the House**.

**The Senate** (Constitution, Article I, Section 3)
- **Two senators from each state** — **100** in all.
- Senators serve **six-year terms**, staggered so **one-third** are elected **every two years**.
- To serve: at least **30 years old**, a citizen for **9 years**, and an inhabitant of the state.
- The **Vice President** is the President of the Senate and votes **only to break a tie**.
- Since the **17th Amendment**, senators are **elected directly by the people** (before that, state legislatures chose them).

**Your representatives:** you have **one representative** and **two senators**. Find your representative by entering your ZIP code at house.gov, and your senators at senate.gov — then write, call or visit about issues you care about.
