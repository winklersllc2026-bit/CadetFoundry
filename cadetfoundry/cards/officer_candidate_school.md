**Officer Candidate School (OCS)** is one way to become a commissioned Army officer. The Army says you can apply **as a civilian, an active-duty enlisted Soldier, or an Army Reserve or Army National Guard Soldier — with at least a bachelor's degree.**

**Federal (active component) OCS requirements** listed by the Minnesota National Guard include:
- a **bachelor's degree** from an accredited college or university
- a minimum **ASVAB GT score of 110**
- commissioning **before your 34th birthday**

There are other requirements too, such as citizenship, a security clearance and a physical, and National Guard state OCS programs have their own rules. **Requirements can change — confirm the current list with a recruiter before you apply.**
