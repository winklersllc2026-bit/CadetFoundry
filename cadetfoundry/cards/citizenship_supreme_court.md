**Article III** of the Constitution creates the judicial branch: "The judicial Power of the United States, shall be vested in **one supreme Court**, and in such inferior Courts as the Congress may from time to time ordain and establish."

**The justices** (supremecourt.gov)
- **Nine justices**: a **Chief Justice** and **eight Associate Justices** (the number of Associate Justices is set by Congress and is currently eight).
- The **President nominates** the justices, and they are appointed **with the advice and consent of the Senate**.
- Federal judges "shall hold their Offices **during good Behaviour**" (Article III) — in practice, they can serve for life unless they retire or are removed.

**Its role** — the Court calls itself the **"guardian and interpreter of the Constitution"** and the **final arbiter of the law**. It can decide whether laws violate the Constitution.
