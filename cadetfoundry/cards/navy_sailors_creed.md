Every Sailor learns the Sailor's Creed:

> I am a United States Sailor.
> I will support and defend the Constitution of the United States of America and I will obey the orders of those appointed over me.
> I represent the fighting spirit of the Navy and those who have gone before me to defend freedom and democracy around the world.
> I proudly serve my country's Navy combat team with honor, courage, and commitment.
> I am committed to excellence and the fair treatment of all.

**What it says, in plain words:** a Sailor's first loyalty is to the Constitution; they follow lawful orders; they carry on the work of the Sailors before them; they live the three core values; and they hold themselves to excellence and treat everyone fairly.

## Quiz
Q: How does the Sailor's Creed begin? | A: I am a United States Sailor | WRONG: I am an American Soldier; I am an American Airman; I am a United States Marine
Q: What does a Sailor promise to support and defend in the Sailor's Creed? | A: The Constitution of the United States of America | WRONG: The President; My ship; The Navy
Q: Whose orders does the Sailor's Creed say a Sailor will obey? | A: Those appointed over me | WRONG: Only my commanding officer's; Anyone older than me; My shipmates'
Q: Which three values does the Sailor's Creed name? | A: Honor, courage, and commitment
Q: How does the Sailor's Creed end? | A: I am committed to excellence and the fair treatment of all | WRONG: I will never leave a fallen comrade; I will never quit; Always faithful, always ready
