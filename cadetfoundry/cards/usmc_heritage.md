Moments every Marine learns about:

- **Guadalcanal (1942)** — in World War II, Marines captured a Japanese airfield on this South Pacific island, using close air support for Marines on the ground.
- **Iwo Jima (1945)** — the flag raising on **Mount Suribachi on February 23, 1945** became a lasting symbol of Marine fighting spirit.
- **Chosin Reservoir (1950)** — in the Korean War, the 1st Marine Division, outnumbered and cut off, fought for **17 days** through snow and temperatures of **−40°F** to break out of encirclement.
- **Huế (1968)** — in the Vietnam War, Marines fought block by block for **33 days** to secure the city.

**Trailblazers:**
- **Navajo Code Talkers** — Marines in World War II who provided an unbreakable code for battlefield communication, crucial to victory over Japan.
- **Montford Point Marines** — more than **20,000** Marines who served in World War II, Korea and Vietnam.
