Marine Corps recruit training lasts **13 weeks**. Those who finish earn the title **Marine**.

**Four phases:**
1. **Foundation** — discipline, structure and working as a unit: in-processing, initial strength testing, basic Marine Corps knowledge.
2. **Immersion** — combat water survival, physical and combat conditioning, martial arts training and academic instruction.
3. **Transformation** — tactical training, team problem solving, marksmanship, more combat training and field exercises.
4. **Transition** — final physical and academic testing and preparation for graduation.

**The Crucible** is a **54-hour** culminating event: hard physical challenges, mental fatigue and little sleep, designed to simulate the hardship of combat. Recruits can only get through it as a team.

**The Eagle, Globe, and Anchor ceremony** is when a recruit first receives the Marine Corps emblem — the moment that signifies they have become a Marine.

Information only — talk to your instructor or a recruiter for anything official.
