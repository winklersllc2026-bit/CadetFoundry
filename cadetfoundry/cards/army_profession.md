**What makes a profession?** (paras 1-2 to 1-4) Professions provide a **vital service to society**, require **expertise built over years** of training and experience, set **standards** for their members, and live by an **ethic** with legal and moral foundations. Examples: medicine, law, engineering, teaching, the clergy — and the military.

**The Army profession** (para 1-6) serves **under civilian authority**, entrusted to **defend the Constitution** and the rights and interests of the American people. It has two communities:
- the **Profession of Arms** — Soldiers of the **Regular Army, Army National Guard and Army Reserve**
- the **Army Civilian Corps**

**Five characteristics of the Army profession** (para 1-7):
1. **Trust** — the foundation of the Army's relationship with the American people. Inside the Army, trust is shared confidence that everyone is competent and can be relied on. Leaders give more authority to people whose judgment they trust.
2. **Honorable service** — supporting the Constitution and the American people **ethically**: obeying the law and legal orders, and **rejecting and reporting illegal, unethical or immoral orders or actions.**
3. **Military expertise** — doing the job ethically, effectively and efficiently.
4. **Stewardship** — caring for the people and resources entrusted to you, and making the organization stronger for the future.
5. **Esprit de corps** — the Army's **winning spirit**: camaraderie, mutual trust and cohesive teamwork — a **never-quit resolve** that carries a team through risk, uncertainty and fear.

**Lifelong learning is a professional obligation** — including giving and accepting coaching, counseling and mentoring.

## Quiz
Q: Name the five characteristics of the Army profession | A: Trust, honorable service, military expertise, stewardship, esprit de corps
Q: What is the foundation of the Army's relationship with the American people? | A: Trust | WRONG: Fear; Money; Tradition
Q: What does honorable service require you to do with illegal or unethical orders? | A: Reject and report them | WRONG: Obey them, then report them later; Ignore them quietly; Pass them on to someone else
Q: What is stewardship? | A: Caring for the people and resources entrusted to you and strengthening the organization | WRONG: Keeping your own equipment for yourself; Spending the unit's money as fast as possible; Leaving decisions to the next leader
Q: Under whose authority does the Army profession serve? | A: Civilian authority | WRONG: Military authority; The authority of the Joint Chiefs alone; The authority of each state governor alone
Q: What are the three parts of the Profession of Arms? | A: The Regular Army, the Army National Guard and the Army Reserve | WRONG: The Army, the Navy and the Air Force; Officers, NCOs and civilians; Active duty, retirees and veterans
Q: Name two things that make a job a profession | A: A vital service to society, expertise built over years, standards for members, an ethic
