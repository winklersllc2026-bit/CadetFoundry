**Electronic communication** is anything sent by computer, phone or another device: texts, emails, chats, direct messages, social media, apps and video calls.

**Online misconduct** includes hazing, bullying, harassment, stalking, retaliation, or anything else that undermines dignity and respect. It counts online the same as it does in person.

**Before you post, "Think, Type, and Post":**
1. **Think** about the message and **who could potentially see it**.
2. **Type** a message that is consistent with your values.
3. **Post** only messages that show **dignity and respect for yourself and others**.

**Protect information, too.** The Army asks everyone to make operations security (OPSEC) a habit — think about what your posts reveal. For you, that means things like your location, your schedule, and other people's information.

A screenshot can last forever. If you wouldn't say it in front of your instructor, your family and the person it's about, don't post it.
