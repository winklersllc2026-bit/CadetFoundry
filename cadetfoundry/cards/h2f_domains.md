The Army's Holistic Health and Fitness (**H2F**) System aims at one thing: **Soldier readiness**. That goal is met when training in all **five domains** is optimized (FM 7-22):

- **Physical readiness** — the ability to meet the physical demands of any duty or combat position, move lethally on the battlefield, accomplish the mission and continue to fight, win, and come home healthy.
- **Nutritional readiness** — the ability to recognize, select, and consume the food and drink needed to meet the demands of any duty or combat position.
- **Mental readiness** — the capacity to adapt successfully in the presence of risk and adversity (para 3-9).
- **Spiritual readiness** — the ability to endure and overcome times of stress, hardship, and tragedy by making meaning of life experiences — from religious, philosophical, or human values (para 3-21).
- **Sleep readiness** — the ability to recognize and put into practice the sleep principles and behaviors that support optimal brain function. Most Soldiers need **7 to 9 hours of sleep every 24 hours** (para 11-3); regularly getting less than 7–8 builds a sleep debt (para 11-11).

The domains work together: poor sleep, nutrition, hydration or stress control all hurt physical training.
