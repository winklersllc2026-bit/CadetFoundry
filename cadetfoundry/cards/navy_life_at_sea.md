**Ships.** On any given day, about **50,000 Sailors** are deployed around the world on roughly **100 ships**.

**Carrier Strike Group (CSG).** The Navy calls it "the ultimate operational formation." It includes an aircraft carrier, cruisers, destroyers, a supply ship and a submarine. An aircraft carrier can have a crew of **5,000**, with jobs from pilots and air traffic controllers to nuclear engineers, IT specialists, chefs and chaplains.

**Amphibious Ready Group.** These ships carry the troops and equipment of a **Marine Expeditionary Unit**. They include amphibious assault ships, dock landing ships and amphibious transport docks.

**Other ships** include hospital ships, littoral combat ships, minesweepers and patrol craft.

**Sea duty and shore duty.** A Sailor is typically assigned to a ship for **three years**, followed by **three years of shore duty**. Ships spend a lot of time at their home port, so you are not at sea the whole time.
- **Deployments** last about **6 to 9 months**.
- Ships typically deploy once every **18 to 24 months**.
- Between deployments, ships often train at sea for **10 days to 2 weeks each month**.

**Living aboard:**
- **Berthing**: your sleeping area, with an assigned **rack** (bed) and storage.
- **Galley**: the kitchen.
- **Mess decks**: where the crew eats and relaxes.
- Many ships have internet, ATMs and mail.

**Time off.** Sailors earn **30 days of paid leave a year**, plus federal holidays.
