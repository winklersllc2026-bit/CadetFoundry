The **Bill of Rights** is the first ten amendments to the Constitution. In plain words (read the exact text in **📚 Books → The Bill of Rights**):

1. **First Amendment** — freedom of religion, speech and the press; the right to assemble peaceably and to petition the government.
2. **Second Amendment** — the right of the people to keep and bear arms.
3. **Third Amendment** — no soldier may be quartered in a house in peacetime without the owner's consent.
4. **Fourth Amendment** — protection against unreasonable searches and seizures; warrants need probable cause.
5. **Fifth Amendment** — rights of a person accused of a crime, including not having to be a witness against yourself, not being tried twice for the same offense, and due process of law.
6. **Sixth Amendment** — in criminal cases, the right to a speedy and public trial by an impartial jury, and to have a lawyer.
7. **Seventh Amendment** — the right to a jury trial in many civil cases.
8. **Eighth Amendment** — no excessive bail or fines, and no cruel and unusual punishment.
9. **Ninth Amendment** — listing some rights in the Constitution doesn't mean the people don't have others.
10. **Tenth Amendment** — powers not given to the federal government, nor denied to the states, belong to the states or to the people.
