**Good leaders create a positive environment.** They build **trust and cohesion**, encourage **initiative**, show they **care for their people**, and build team spirit (**esprit de corps**) by connecting the team to its traditions and history.

**Be approachable.** Approachable leaders:
- encourage open, honest (**candid**) communication
- **respect other people's opinions**, even ones they disagree with
- stay **objective when they hear bad news** — so people keep telling them the truth

**Culture vs. climate:**
- **Culture** is long-lasting: the shared attitudes, values, goals and practices of a whole organization over time.
- **Climate** is shorter-term: **how people think and feel about their team right now.** It changes as people come and go.

A positive climate means people are engaged in their duties, work together as a team, and show respect for each other. Every leader — even a team leader — shapes the climate of their team.
