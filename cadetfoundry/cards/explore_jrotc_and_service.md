**First: you owe nothing.** JROTC participation **does not incur any military obligation**. Most Cadets never serve, and that's exactly as it should be — JROTC's purpose is citizenship, service and responsibility.

**If you do choose to serve, your JROTC years can count:**
- **Advanced rank on enlistment** — under military policy (DODI 1205.13), a student who successfully completes **at least two years** of JROTC is entitled to enlist at **no less than E-2**. The services **may** award **E-3** for **three years**.
- **Service academy nominations** — the military department secretaries may nominate **up to 20 honor graduates** from JROTC units designated as **honor schools** for appointment to the service academies.

**Research note:** studies have linked JROTC participation with positive outcomes for those who go on to enlist.

Talk with your instructor and a recruiter about the exact rules for any branch — and only when *you* decide you want to know.
