**Signs of choking:** the person often **grabs their throat**. They may be **unable to speak**, have trouble breathing, or turn bluish.

**Ask: "Are you choking? Can you speak?"**
- If they're **coughing forcefully and can speak**, don't interfere — **encourage them to keep coughing.**
- If they **can't speak or are struggling to breathe**, act:

**Back blows and abdominal thrusts** (for adults and children over 1 year):
1. **5 back blows** — stand behind the person, lean them forward, and give firm blows with the heel of your hand **between the shoulder blades**.
2. **5 abdominal thrusts** (the Heimlich maneuver) — stand behind them with your arms around their waist. Put your **fist just above the belly button**, grab it with your other hand, and give a **quick, upward and inward thrust**.
3. **Repeat** 5 and 5 until the object comes out or the person becomes unconscious.

For someone who is pregnant or very large, use **chest thrusts** instead of abdominal thrusts.

**If they become unconscious:** shout for help and have someone **call 911**. Lay them on a firm, flat surface and **begin CPR**, checking the mouth for the object before giving breaths.
