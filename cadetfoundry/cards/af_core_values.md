The Air Force's three core values:

- **Integrity First** — an Airman is a person of integrity, courage and conviction: able to control their impulses and to exercise courage, honesty and accountability "to do what is right even when no one is looking."
- **Service Before Self** — professional duties come before personal desires. It takes the discipline to follow rules, self-control, and respect for the beliefs, authority and worth of others.
- **Excellence In All We Do** — striving for continual improvement in yourself and in your service.

Air Force JROTC is grounded in these same three values.

**In class:** doing your own work on a test when the teacher steps out (Integrity First), giving up free time to help set up an event (Service Before Self), and redoing a uniform inspection item until it's right (Excellence).
