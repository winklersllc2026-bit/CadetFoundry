Marine Corps enlisted ranks from E-1 to E-9:

- **E-1** Private
- **E-2** Private First Class (PFC)
- **E-3** Lance Corporal (LCpl)
- **E-4** Corporal (Cpl)
- **E-5** Sergeant (Sgt)
- **E-6** Staff Sergeant (SSgt)
- **E-7** Gunnery Sergeant (GySgt)
- **E-8** Master Sergeant (MSgt) **or** First Sergeant
- **E-9** Master Gunnery Sergeant (MGySgt) **or** Sergeant Major (SgtMaj)

At E-8 and E-9 there are two ranks at the same pay grade. They're paid the same but have different jobs — and a **Sergeant Major** is the higher rank than a Master Gunnery Sergeant. The senior enlisted Marine is the **Sergeant Major of the Marine Corps**.

In the Marine Corps, noncommissioned officer (NCO) rank begins at **Corporal**, an E-4. (In the Air Force, NCO rank starts one grade higher, at Staff Sergeant.) A Marine's job is a **military occupational specialty** (MOS).
