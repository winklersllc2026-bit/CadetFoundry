Ways the Air Force describes to combine college and service:

- **Air Force ROTC (AFROTC)** — attend the college of your choice, with scholarship opportunities that can cover tuition plus stipends, and graduate as an officer in the **Air Force or Space Force**.
- **U.S. Air Force Academy** — tuition fully covered; graduates become officers in the Air Force or Space Force.
- **Community College of the Air Force** — enlisted Airmen who join with a high school diploma or GED automatically begin earning credits toward an **associate degree in applied science**.

**While still in high school:**
- **Junior ROTC** — taught by retired Air Force instructors.
- **Flight Academy** — a fully paid program that teaches eligible Cadets to fly with Air Force pilot instructors.

Every option has requirements and deadlines that change. Ask your instructor and the official program sites.

Q: What degree do enlisted Airmen start earning credits toward through the Community College of the Air Force? | A: An associate degree in applied science | WRONG: A bachelor of arts; A master's degree; A high school diploma
Q: Which Air Force program for high school students teaches Cadets to fly? | A: The Flight Academy | WRONG: The Community College of the Air Force; Air Force ROTC; Basic Military Training
Q: What does AFROTC let you do? | A: Attend the college of your choice and graduate as an officer | WRONG: Skip college and enlist as a sergeant; Attend the Air Force Academy tuition-free; Train as a pilot instead of going to college
