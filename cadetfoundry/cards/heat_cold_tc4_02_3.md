**Avoid heat injury** (para 2-45):
- **Acclimatize:** it takes at least **3 to 5 days**, and up to **two weeks** to fully adjust to heat.
- **Drink water:** depending on heat and activity, **½ to 1½ quarts an hour** — and up to **3 gallons (12 quarts) a day** in hot, dry climates.
- Use the **work/rest cycles** your leaders set.
- **Eat all your meals** — they replace the salt you sweat out.
- Sunscreen on all exposed skin.

**Avoid cold injury** (paras 2-46 to 2-51):
- Wear clothing in **loose layers**; keep it clean and dry; **change wet or damp clothes** as soon as possible.
- Loosen or remove layers when working hard, so you don't sweat.
- Wear headgear — the body loses a lot of heat through the head.
- Keep moving; exercise toes, feet, fingers and hands if you're stuck in a small space.
- **No alcohol** (you lose heat faster) and **no tobacco** (less blood flow to the skin).
- Eat all meals and drink plenty — **dark yellow urine means you're not drinking enough.** You can dehydrate in the cold too.
- **Buddies watch each other** for frostbite. **Never rewarm frozen body parts** except under medical supervision.
- Don't rub your face and ears to warm them; cover them with your hands. No face camouflage when the wind chill is **−10 °F or below** — it hides frostbite.

**Carbon monoxide** (paras 2-53, 2-54) — colorless, odorless, tasteless, and it can kill. **Never sleep in a vehicle with the engine running** or use engine exhaust for heat. Ventilate living spaces that have fuel-burning heaters.

**Sleep** (para 2-62): **7 to 8 hours** in each 24 hours keeps your performance up. Less than that, and it drops.
