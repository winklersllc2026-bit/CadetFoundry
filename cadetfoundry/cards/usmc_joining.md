**Enlisting in the Marine Corps** (marines.com):
- **Age 17 to 28.**
- A **high school diploma or GED**.
- A **U.S. citizen or legal resident**.
- A criminal background check with **no felony convictions**.
- An **ASVAB score of 31 or higher** — **50 or higher** with a GED or a nontraditional diploma.

**The Initial Strength Test (IST)** comes before recruit training:
- Men: **3 pull-ups or 34 push-ups** (2 minutes) and a **1.5-mile run in 13:30**.
- Women: **1 pull-up or 15 push-ups** (2 minutes) and a **1.5-mile run in 15:00**.
- Everyone: a **40-second plank**.

**Becoming a Marine officer:**
- A **four-year college degree** at commissioning, **U.S. citizenship**, and **at least 20 years old**.
- **NROTC (Marine option)** — a path that starts in high school.
- **Platoon Leaders Class (PLC)** — for college students: two **six-week** summer sessions (freshmen and sophomores) or one **ten-week** session (juniors).
- **Officer Candidate Course (OCC)** — a **10-week** program at **Quantico, Virginia**, for college graduates.
- The **U.S. Naval Academy** — with the Marine Corps option.
- Every path commissions a **Second Lieutenant**, who then goes to **The Basic School** at Quantico.

Requirements change — confirm them on marines.com or with a recruiter or Officer Selection Officer.
