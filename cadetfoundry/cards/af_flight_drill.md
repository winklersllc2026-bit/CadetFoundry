**The flight** is the basic drill unit. It has at least **two but not more than four elements**. The flight commander, flight sergeant and guide hold the key positions.

**The guide** sets the **direction and cadence** of the march. Unless told otherwise, the guide is on the **right**. "GUIDE LEFT" moves the guide to the left. The guide and flight commander trade places by passing right shoulder to right shoulder.

**"FALL IN":**
- The guide falls in first.
- The first element leader falls in on the guide's left.
- The other element leaders line up behind the first, **40 inches** apart.
- Everyone else fills in to the left and does an automatic dress right dress.

**Sizing.** The tallest Airmen and Guardians go to the **front and right**.

**Numbering.** Members are numbered from right to left in line and from front to rear in column. The **element leader is always number one**, and the guide has no number.

**"Dress Right, DRESS":**
- Everyone except the last person in each element raises the left arm straight out from the shoulder.
- Everyone except the guide and the element leaders behind the first turns their head **45 degrees** right.
- The leading person in each file moves until their shoulder touches the fingertips of the person to their right.
- "Ready, FRONT" drops the arms and brings the heads back.

**"Open Ranks, MARCH"** (in line, normal interval only):
- The fourth rank stands fast.
- The third, second and first ranks step forward **one, two and three** paces.
- That leaves **64 inches** between ranks for an inspection.

**"Count, OFF"** in line: each file calls out its number in turn, starting with the element leaders at "ONE."

**"Close, MARCH" / "Extend, MARCH"** switch the flight between close and normal interval.

## Quiz
Q: How many elements are in an Air Force flight? | A: At least two, but not more than four | WRONG: Exactly three; At least four, but not more than six; Only one
Q: Who sets the direction and cadence of the march? | A: The guide | WRONG: The flight commander; The element leader; The last member of the column
Q: When sized in column, where do the tallest members go? | A: To the front and right | WRONG: To the rear and left; To the center; To the front and left
Q: Who is always number one in an element? | A: The element leader | WRONG: The guide; The newest member; The flight commander
Q: At open ranks, how far apart are the ranks? | A: 64 inches
Q: At open ranks, which rank stands fast? | A: The fourth rank
