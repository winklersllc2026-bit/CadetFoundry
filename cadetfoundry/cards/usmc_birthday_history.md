**"On November 10, 1775, a Corps of Marines was created by a resolution of Continental Congress."** — Marine Corps Order No. 47, 1921

- **Tun Tavern:** Captain **Samuel Nicholas** set up the first Marine recruiting headquarters at Tun Tavern in Philadelphia.
- **1776:** five companies of Marines made the Corps' first amphibious raid, at Fort Nassau in the Bahamas.
- **Order No. 47:** in 1921 the Commandant, Major General **John A. Lejeune**, ordered that a message about the Corps' history be read to every command each November 10. Marines still read it at every birthday celebration.

**Nicknames with history:**
- **Leatherneck** — from the high collar Marines wore around the time of the Battle of Derna (1805), the "shores of Tripoli" in the Marines' Hymn.
- **Devil Dogs** — what German soldiers called Marines after the Battle of **Belleau Wood** in World War I (1918).
