**Why we drill** (para 1001): to move units in a standard, orderly way; to teach discipline through precision and automatic response to orders; and to build confidence by giving and following proper commands. The NJROTC Drill Manual was written to **mirror the Marine Corps Drill and Ceremonies Manual**.

**Training Time Out (TTO)** (para 1000.5): anyone — Cadet or instructor — can call a TTO if they're worried about safety. Drill stops, the problem is fixed, and training continues if possible. A TTO is the right way to get relief from pain, heat stress or other serious discomfort.

**Attention** (para 2000): heels together, feet out at **45 degrees**, legs straight but knees not locked, thumbs along the trouser seams, head and eyes to the front, silent and still.

**Rests** (para 2001) — four of them, from attention:
- **Parade rest** — left foot 12 inches to the left, hands clasped at the small of the back. Silent and still.
- **At ease** — right foot stays in place; you may move but not talk.
- **Rest** — right foot stays in place; you may move and talk.
- **Fall out** — leave ranks but stay nearby.

**Facings** (para 2003): Right face, left face and about face are **two-count** movements done at quick time.

**Hand salute** (para 2004): on "Hand, SALUTE," raise the right hand until the tip of the forefinger touches the headdress above and slightly right of the right eye, palm visible, upper arm parallel to the deck. "Ready, TWO" returns you to attention.

Q: At attention, at what angle are your feet turned out? | A: 45 degrees
Q: At parade rest, how far does the left foot move? | A: 12 inches
Q: Which rest position lets you move and talk but keeps your right foot in place? | A: Rest | WRONG: At Ease; Parade Rest; Fall Out
Q: How many counts is a facing movement? | A: Two
Q: Which service's drill manual does the NJROTC Drill Manual mirror? | A: The Marine Corps | WRONG: The Army; The Air Force; The Coast Guard
