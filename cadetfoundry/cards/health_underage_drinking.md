**Alcohol is the most common drug used by people younger than 21** in the United States.

**About 4,000 young people under 21 die from excessive alcohol use each year.**

**What underage drinking can lead to** (CDC):
- **School problems** — more absences and lower grades.
- **Risky choices** — drinking and driving, and misusing other drugs, including mixing them with alcohol.
- **Violence and injury** — including car crashes, burns, falls and drowning.
- **Slower brain development**, which may cause **memory problems**, and not growing as expected.
- **Higher risk of problem drinking later in life** — starting early raises the risk of alcohol use disorder.

**How common it is:**
- About **9%** of high school students reported **binge drinking** in the past month.
- About **16%** rode with a driver who had been drinking.

**What you can do:** have a simple "no thanks" ready, stick with friends who don't drink, never ride with a driver who has been drinking — call a parent or trusted adult instead — and talk to a trusted adult if alcohol is causing problems for you or someone you care about.
