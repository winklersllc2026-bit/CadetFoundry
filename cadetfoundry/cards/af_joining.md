**Three ways to serve in the Air Force:**
- **Active Duty**: full-time.
- **Air National Guard**: part-time, serving in the state you choose.
- **Air Force Reserve**: part-time, while you build a civilian career.

**Enlisted steps:**
1. Apply through a recruiter.
2. Take the aptitude test (the **ASVAB**).
3. Pass physical and mental screening.
4. Prepare for Basic Military Training.

**Who can enlist (Active Duty):**
- **Age**: at least **17** and not yet **42**.
- **Education**: a high school diploma, a **GED**, or **15 college semester hours**. High school seniors can start the process before they graduate.
- **ASVAB**: the minimum score depends on your education and runs from **31 to 65**.
- **GED in the Guard or Reserve**: you must be at least **18** and wait for a GED slot.

**Officers:**
- Need a **four-year degree**, or be within **365 days** of finishing one.
- Officer candidates don't take the ASVAB. Most take the **Air Force Officer Qualifying Test (AFOQT)** instead. It covers verbal and math skills, plus pilot and navigation aptitude for flyers.
- AFOQT scores are percentiles from **1 to 99**.
- You can take the AFOQT **only twice**, so study.
- After acceptance, you wait in the **Delayed Entry Program (DEP)** until Officer Training School.

**Tattoos and piercings.** The Air Force has strict rules about body modifications. Think carefully about placement, size and content before getting one.
