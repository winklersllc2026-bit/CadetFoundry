The Space Force's fitness test is the **Human Performance Assessment (HPA)**. The rules are in **SPFMAN 36-2905, Human Performance and Readiness**.

**How often.** Starting **January 1, 2026**, every Guardian takes **two HPAs a year**.

**What's on the test:**
- **Cardio**: a **2-mile run** or the **20-meter High Aerobic Multi-Shuttle Run (HAMR)**. At least **one of the two tests each year must be the 2-mile run**.
- **Strength**: tempo push-ups or hand-release push-ups.
- **Endurance**: sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunches, or a timed forearm plank.
- **Body composition**: a **waist-to-height ratio** measurement.

**Scoring.** As announced in September 2025, the HPA is scored on an **80-point scale**. A Guardian needs **60 or higher** to be considered physically ready. Fitness scores also appear on Guardians' performance briefs.

**Every day, not just test day.** The Space Force wants Guardians to **exercise regularly** and build lasting habits, not cram before a test. Its **Holistic Health Approach** covers eight areas of "Total Force Fitness":
1. Physical
2. Financial
3. Spiritual
4. Preventive care
5. Environmental
6. Nutritional
7. Psychological
8. Social

**Guardian Resilience Teams** at every Space Force base help with resilience and healthy habits.

Fitness standards change often. Check the latest official guidance before you test.
