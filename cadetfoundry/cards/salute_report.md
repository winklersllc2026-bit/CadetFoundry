title: The SALUTE Report
topic: Reports
source: TC 3-21.76, Ranger Handbook (para. 2-16, Table 2-7)
---
Report what you see of the enemy **quickly, completely and accurately** with **SALUTE**:

- **S — Size**: how many. *Seven enemy personnel.*
- **A — Activity**: what they are doing. *Traveling southwest.*
- **L — Location**: where — a grid. *GA123456.*
- **U — Unit / Uniform**: who they are, what they wear. *Olive-drab uniforms with a red six-point star on the left shoulder.*
- **T — Time**: when you saw it. *210200 January 16.*
- **E — Equipment**: what they carry. *One machine gun and one rocket launcher.*

Report what you **saw**, not what you guess. Try to add a field sketch of anything of military importance (targets, obstacles, positions) — but people, weapons and equipment go in the SALUTE report, not the sketch.

## Quiz
Q: What does SALUTE stand for? | A: Size, Activity, Location, Unit/Uniform, Time, Equipment | WRONG: Speed, Altitude, Location, Unit, Terrain, Enemy; Size, Ammunition, Leader, Uniform, Time, Equipment; Situation, Action, Location, Unit, Terrain, Escape
Q: In a SALUTE report, what goes under "A"? | A: Activity — what the enemy is doing | WRONG: Ammunition — how much the enemy has; Altitude — how high the enemy is; Armor — how many vehicles
Q: In a SALUTE report, what goes under "U"? | A: Unit or uniform — who they are and what they wear | WRONG: Urgency — how fast to respond; Utilities — water and power nearby; Unknown — what you couldn't see
Q: In a SALUTE report, how do you give the location? | A: As a grid coordinate | WRONG: As a street address; As a compass direction only; As a description, like "over by the trees"
Q: What is a SALUTE report for? | A: Reporting enemy information quickly, completely and accurately | WRONG: Requesting a medical evacuation; Reporting water and ammunition after a fight; Saluting the commander
