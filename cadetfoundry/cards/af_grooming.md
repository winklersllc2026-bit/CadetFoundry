These are the standards for **Airmen and Guardians** in the Air Force and Space Force. AFJROTC Cadets follow their own program's guidance, so ask your instructor.

**All hair** (para 3.1.1): conservative, clean, well-groomed and professional. It can't exceed the length and bulk standards, interfere with headgear, helmets or masks, look lopsided, or touch the eyebrows (except women's bangs). Dyes must give **natural colors**: brown, blonde, brunette, natural red, gray or black.

**Not authorized** (paras 3.1.1.3–3.1.1.4): burgundy, purple, orange, fluorescent or neon hair; ombré; Mohawks, mullets or etched designs.

**Men** (para 3.1.2):
- A tapered look on the sides and back, conforming to the shape of the head.
- No more than **2½ inches of bulk**, and ¼ inch at the natural termination point.
- Only closely cut or shaved hair on the back of the neck may touch the collar; hair can't stick out under the front of headgear.
- Shaved heads, high-and-tight and flattop cuts are authorized.
- Sideburns are straight and even, no lower than the bottom of the ear opening.

Standards change through updates and policy memos. The current DAFI and your chain of command have the final word.
