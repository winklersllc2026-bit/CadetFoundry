**Birthday — September 18, 1947.** After about 40 years as part of the Army, the Air Force became a separate service. President Harry S. Truman signed the **National Security Act of 1947** on July 26, 1947; the Department of the Air Force became effective **September 18, 1947**, when the first Secretary of the Air Force, W. Stuart Symington, took the oath of office.

**Mission:** "To fly, fight, and win…airpower anytime, anywhere."

**Five core functions:**
- Air superiority
- Global strike
- Rapid global mobility
- Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance
- Command and control

The Air Force's work reaches across air, space and cyberspace. The Department of the Air Force is led by a civilian **Secretary of the Air Force**; the **Chief of Staff of the Air Force** is the senior officer and a member of the Joint Chiefs of Staff.
