**The flag represents the living country and is considered a living thing** (para I-6).

**The union** — the blue field with the stars — is the **honor point** and the flag's own right (para I-7).
- On display, the union is always **at the top and to the flag's own right** — **the observer's left**.
- When carried, the flag flies **free** — never stretched flat or carried horizontally.
- With other flags, the U.S. flag is on the **marching right** (the flag's own right), or in **front of the center** of a line of flags.
- In a group of state or local flags, the U.S. flag is **in the center and highest**.
- With flags of other nations: **separate staffs of the same height**, flags of about **equal size**, the U.S. flag to the right.

**Never drapery** (para I-8): the flag is hung flat against a wall or flown free — never draped over a platform, tied in a bow, or used to cover a desk. Use **bunting** (blue at the top) for decoration.

**Flag vs. Color** (para I-9): a **Color** is a U.S. flag **trimmed with golden yellow fringe** on three sides, mainly for **indoor display**. A plain flag is saluted only during raising and lowering.

**U.S. flag sizes the Army uses:** **garrison** (20 × 38 feet — holidays and important occasions), **post** (flown daily), **field**, and **storm** (bad weather).

**Other flags:** a **guidon** is the **swallow-tailed** flag carried by companies, batteries and troops; a **pennant** is triangular; an **ensign** flies from aircraft, ships and boats.

## Quiz
Q: What part of the flag is the honor point? | A: The union — the blue field with the stars
Q: When the flag hangs on a wall, which side is the union on for someone looking at it? | A: The observer's left, at the top | WRONG: The observer's right, at the top; The observer's left, at the bottom; The center
Q: Where does the U.S. flag go in a group of state flags? | A: In the center and at the highest point | WRONG: At the far left, lower than the others; At the end of the line; Behind the state flags
Q: With other flags in a line, which side is the U.S. flag on? | A: Its own right — the marching right | WRONG: Its own left; In the center, at the same height; At the end on the observer's right
Q: What is a Color? | A: A U.S. flag trimmed with golden yellow fringe, mainly for indoor display | WRONG: A flag carried by a company; A small flag for cars; A flag flown only at night
Q: What is a guidon? | A: The swallow-tailed flag carried by a company, battery or troop | WRONG: The national flag with gold fringe; A flag flown on ships; The largest flag on post
Q: Which U.S. flag size is flown on holidays and important occasions? | A: The garrison flag | WRONG: The storm flag; The post flag; The field flag
Q: What should you use instead of the flag to decorate a platform? | A: Bunting | WRONG: The national flag; Balloons; Paper streamers
