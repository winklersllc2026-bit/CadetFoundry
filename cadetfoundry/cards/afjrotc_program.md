**AFJROTC mission:** "Develop citizens of character."

**Goals:** instill the values of citizenship, service to the United States, personal responsibility, and a sense of accomplishment.

- About **843 units** and **91,000 Cadets** in grades 8–12, in the United States, Guam and some overseas schools. Headquarters is at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama.
- The curriculum has three parts: **Aerospace Science, Leadership Education, and Health and Wellness**.
- Activities include drill team, color guard, Raiders, unmanned aircraft systems, CyberPatriot, StellarXplorers, rocketry, robotics, academic bowl, marksmanship and archery.
- Cadets contribute more than **1.6 million hours** of community service a year.
- The **Chief of Staff of the Air Force Flight Academy** scholarship offers eligible Cadets private pilot training.
- **Space Force JROTC:** after the Space Force was created in December 2019, ten AFJROTC units became Space Force JROTC units.

AFJROTC is **not a recruiting program**, and joining it creates **no military service obligation**.
