**Most e-cigarettes contain nicotine, which is highly addictive.**

**Nicotine and a teenage brain:** nicotine can harm the parts of a teen's brain that control **attention, learning, mood and impulse control** — the same things you use in class, in sports and as a leader.

**Most middle and high school students who vape want to quit.** You wouldn't be the only one.

**Free help to quit** (from the National Cancer Institute's Smokefree Teen):
- **SmokefreeTXT for Teens** — a free text-message program for ages 13–17, with 24/7 encouragement. Sign up or opt out any time.
- **QuitPlan** — build your own quit plan at teen.smokefree.gov.
- **LiveHelp** — chat with a trained counselor at livehelp.cancer.gov.

**Quitting tips from Smokefree Teen:**
- Quitting can be tough, but you are tougher.
- **Know your triggers** and have a plan for them.
- Vaping doesn't fix sad or stressed feelings — find healthy ways to manage your mood (see the Managing Stress card).

A trusted adult — a parent, your instructor, a school nurse or counselor — can help too.
