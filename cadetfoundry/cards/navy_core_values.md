The Navy's three core values are **Honor, Courage and Commitment**. Each one is tied to a line of the oath every Sailor takes.

- **Honor** — "I will bear true faith and allegiance." Be honest and truthful, keep your word, take responsibility for your actions, make honest recommendations and accept good ideas from juniors, and deliver bad news even when it's unpopular.
- **Courage** — "I will support and defend." The moral and mental strength to do what is right, even in the face of personal or professional adversity, and to make decisions in the best interest of the Navy and the nation without regard to personal consequences.
- **Commitment** — "I will obey the orders." Respect up and down the chain of command, care for your people, treat every person with dignity, and work together as a team to improve "the quality of our work, our people and ourselves."

The Marine Corps shares the same three core values.

**In class:** owning a mistake (Honor), speaking up when a teammate is being treated badly (Courage), and finishing your part of a team project (Commitment).
