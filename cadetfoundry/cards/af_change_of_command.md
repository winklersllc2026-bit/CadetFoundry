**Why hold a change of command?** So that everyone in the unit can **witness** command passing from one officer to the next. It should be "official, formal, brief, and conducted with great dignity."

**Order of events:**
1. Formation of troops
2. Presentation of troops and honors
3. Inspection
4. Decorations and awards, if any
5. The formal change of command
6. March in review

**Passing the flag or guidon:**
1. The order is read.
2. The **outgoing commander** salutes the presiding officer and says **"I relinquish command."**
3. The outgoing commander hands the flag or guidon to the **presiding officer**.
4. The presiding officer hands it to the **incoming commander**.
5. The incoming commander salutes and says **"I assume command."**

Passing the flag is a visible symbol of command authority moving from one leader to the next.

**The color guard:**
- When practical, it is **two NCOs** who carry the flags and **two guards**.
- Carrying the flags is an **honor** given only to responsible NCOs.
- The **senior flagbearer carries the U.S. flag** and commands the color guard.
- The Air Force or Space Force flag goes on the **marching left** of the U.S. flag.
- The Air Force or Space Force flag is **never carried without the U.S. flag**.
- The flagbearers are unarmed. Guards carry rifles but **no ammunition** for ceremonies.
- The color guard **never does an about face or to the rear march**. It turns with a right or left about instead.

## Quiz
Q: What is the main purpose of a change of command ceremony? | A: To let subordinates witness command passing from one officer to another | WRONG: To promote the outgoing commander; To retire the unit's colors; To welcome new recruits to the unit
Q: What does the outgoing commander say? | A: I relinquish command | WRONG: I assume command; Sir, I report; Present, arms
Q: What does the incoming commander say? | A: I assume command | WRONG: I relinquish command; Command accepted; Sir, I report
Q: Who carries the U.S. flag in an Air Force color guard? | A: The senior flagbearer | WRONG: The left rifle guard; The organizational flag bearer; The right rifle guard
Q: Can the Air Force flag be carried without the U.S. flag? | A: No, never
Q: Which movements does a color guard never perform? | A: About face and to the rear march | WRONG: Forward march and halt; Present arms and order arms; Mark time and forward march
