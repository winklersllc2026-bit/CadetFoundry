Every physical training session has three parts: **preparation, activities, recovery** (FM 7-22, paras 6-23 to 6-26).

**Preparation Drill (PD)** — 10 exercises, done first. It's meant to lower the chance of a musculoskeletal injury during harder training such as the Army Fitness Test (ATP 7-22.02, para 3-1):
1. Bend and Reach
2. Rear Lunge
3. High Jumper
4. Rower
5. Squat Bender
6. Windmill
7. Forward Lunge
8. Prone Row
9. Bent-Leg Body Twist
10. Push-Up

**Recovery Drill (RD)** — 8 exercises, done last. It gradually and safely tapers off activity to bring the body back to its pre-exercise state (para 1-25):
1. Overhead Arm Pull
2. Rear Lunge
3. Extend and Flex
4. Thigh Stretch
5. Single-Leg Over
6. Groin Stretch
7. Calf Stretch
8. Hamstring Stretch

**PMCS** (Preventive Maintenance Checks and Services) is the other recovery drill: a self-check for musculoskeletal problems, so you catch them before they become chronic (para 1-25). Recovery continues all day with good nutrition and sleep.

Learn the movements from your cadre — demonstration videos are on the Central Army Registry (ATP 7-22.02, para 3-2).
