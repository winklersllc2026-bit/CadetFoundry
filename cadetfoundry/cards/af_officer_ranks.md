The Air Force and the Space Force use the same officer ranks, O-1 to O-10:

- **O-1** Second Lieutenant (2d Lt)
- **O-2** First Lieutenant (1st Lt)
- **O-3** Captain (Capt)
- **O-4** Major (Maj)
- **O-5** Lieutenant Colonel (Lt Col)
- **O-6** Colonel (Col)
- **O-7** Brigadier General (Brig Gen)
- **O-8** Major General (Maj Gen)
- **O-9** Lieutenant General (Lt Gen)
- **O-10** General (Gen)

These are the same titles the Army and Marine Corps use, with slightly different abbreviations. Officers are grouped as **company grade** (O-1 to O-3), **field grade** (O-4 to O-6) and **general officers** (O-7 and up). General of the Air Force is reserved for wartime.
