Stress is normal. These are healthy ways the CDC lists to handle it.

**Take care of your mind:**
- **Take breaks from news and social media.**
- **Make time to unwind** — deep breathing, stretching, journaling, time outdoors, or something you enjoy.
- **Practice gratitude daily** — write down specific things you're thankful for.
- **Connect with others** — talk with people you trust; stay involved with community or faith groups.

**Take care of your body:**
- **Get enough sleep** — keep the same bedtime and wake time.
- **Move more** — start small; even 20 to 30 minutes at a time counts.
- **Eat well** — fruits, vegetables, lean protein, whole grains; limit unhealthy fats, salt and added sugar.
- **Avoid smoking, vaping and other tobacco**, and don't misuse drugs or take anyone else's medication.

**If you or someone you know is struggling or in crisis:** call or text **988**, or chat at 988lifeline.org — free, confidential, 24/7. You can also talk to a parent, your instructor, or a school counselor.
