**Brown v. Board of Education — May 17, 1954**
- The Supreme Court ruled that **state-sanctioned segregation of public schools violated the 14th Amendment.**
- Chief Justice **Earl Warren** delivered the **unanimous** ruling: "**Separate educational facilities are inherently unequal.**"
- It ended the "**separate but equal**" principle from *Plessy v. Ferguson* (1896).

**The Civil Rights Act — signed July 2, 1964, by President Lyndon Johnson**
- **Prohibited discrimination in public places** — equal access to places like hotels, restaurants and theaters regardless of race, color, religion or national origin.
- **Made employment discrimination illegal** (Title VII) — on the basis of **race, color, religion, sex or national origin**.
- Created the **Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC)** to enforce it.

**The Voting Rights Act — signed August 6, 1965, by President Lyndon Johnson**
- An act "**to enforce the fifteenth amendment**" — it **outlawed discriminatory voting practices**, including **literacy tests**.
- National attention to the attack on **peaceful marchers in Selma, Alabama**, helped push Congress and the President to act.

Citizens like the Selma marchers used their First Amendment rights — to speak, assemble peaceably and petition the government — to push for these changes.
