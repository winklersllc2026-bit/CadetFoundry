**How much:** teens **13–17 years** need **8–10 hours** of sleep. Adults 18–60 need 7 or more hours.

**Habits that help (CDC):**
- Go to bed and get up at the same time every day.
- Keep your bedroom quiet, relaxing and at a cool temperature.
- Turn off electronic devices at least 30 minutes before bedtime.
- Avoid large meals before bedtime.
- Avoid caffeine in the afternoon or evening.
- Exercise regularly and eat a healthy diet.

If sleep problems keep up, talk with a parent or guardian, the school nurse, or a doctor.
