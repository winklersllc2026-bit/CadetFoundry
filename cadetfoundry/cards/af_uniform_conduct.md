**The philosophy.** The uniform will be **"plain, distinctive, and standardized."** Anything the instruction doesn't list as authorized is unauthorized.

**Five elements of the standard:**
1. **Neatness**
2. **Cleanliness**
3. **Safety**
4. **Uniformity**
5. **Military image**

Every Airman and Guardian, regardless of rank, is responsible for **respectfully enforcing** the standards.

**In uniform, while walking or in formation:**
- **No eating** while walking. Drinking a beverage is allowed.
- **No smoking** outside designated areas.
- **No texting or phone calls** while walking, except in emergencies or for official notifications.
- Earbuds and headphones are for **official duties** only. The exceptions are public transportation and personal workouts in PT gear.
- **No public displays of affection.** Holding a young child's hand is fine, and so is a brief hug at a graduation, promotion or homecoming.

**Jewelry:**
- **One bracelet**, no wider than ½ inch. No ankle bracelets.
- **One watch**.
- **No more than three rings.** A wedding set counts as one.
- **Necklaces** must never be visible.
- **Earrings**: only small matching studs for women, worn in the lower earlobes. Men may not wear earrings in uniform.

**Sunglasses** are never worn around the neck or on top of the head.

**Tattoos:**
- None on the head, face, tongue, lips, eyes or scalp.
- No content that is obscene, extremist, racist, sexist or religiously discriminatory, anywhere on the body. Covering it with a bandage or makeup isn't allowed.
- One small neck tattoo is allowed: no more than **1 inch**, and only behind the ear opening.

AFJROTC Cadets follow their unit's cadet uniform guide. Your instructor has the final word.
