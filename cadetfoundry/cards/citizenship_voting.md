**To vote in U.S. federal elections, you must:**
- be a U.S. citizen
- be 18 years old on or before Election Day
- meet your state's residency requirements
- be registered to vote by your state's deadline (North Dakota doesn't require registration)

**Before you turn 18:**
- In almost every state, you can **register before you turn 18** if you'll be 18 by Election Day.
- Some states let **17-year-olds who will be 18 by Election Day vote in primaries.**

Rules differ by state — check your state's election office or vote.gov.
