**Four types of ceremonies:**
1. **Reviews**
2. **Decorations** (awards)
3. **Parades**, either ceremonial or street
4. **Funerals and special ceremonies**

The **commander of troops** is the senior officer taking part. They are responsible for the organization, uniform and equipment of the units in the ceremony.

**A review has four parts:**
1. **Formation** of the unit. The adjutant dresses the line, presents the units and reports "Sir, the parade is formed."
2. **Presentation and honors** to the reviewing officer.
3. **Inspection**, also called trooping the line.
4. **March in review**, on the command **"PASS IN REVIEW."** Leaders salute and units execute **eyes right** from **six paces before** the reviewing party until **six paces beyond** it.

**Joint reviews.** When other services march with an NJROTC unit, the order from the head of the column is **Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard**.

**Awards in a review:**
- On "Persons to be decorated and all colors, CENTER, MARCH," the people receiving awards form a single rank, with the **highest award on the right**.
- The reviewing officer pins the award **over the left breast pocket**, presents the citation and shakes hands.
- The person who was decorated then **salutes** the reviewing officer.

**Street parades** are run by civilian organizations and are less formal. A **grand marshal** organizes and directs them. The manual calls a parade a chance to bring credit on your school and unit.
