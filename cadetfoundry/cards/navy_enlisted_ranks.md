Navy enlisted ranks from E-1 to E-9:

- **E-1** Seaman Recruit (SR)
- **E-2** Seaman Apprentice (SA)
- **E-3** Seaman (SN)
- **E-4** Petty Officer Third Class (PO3)
- **E-5** Petty Officer Second Class (PO2)
- **E-6** Petty Officer First Class (PO1)
- **E-7** Chief Petty Officer (CPO)
- **E-8** Senior Chief Petty Officer (SCPO)
- **E-9** Master Chief Petty Officer (MCPO) — plus fleet and command master chiefs

The senior enlisted Sailor in the whole Navy is the **Master Chief Petty Officer of the Navy (MCPON)**.

**Rank vs. rate:** a Sailor's job specialty is called a **rate** (the Army and Marine Corps say "military occupational specialty"). A specialty mark in the center of a rating badge shows the wearer's rate. Petty officers are the Navy's equivalent of noncommissioned officers, starting at **Petty Officer Third Class**. Gold stripes on the badge show 12 or more years of good conduct.
