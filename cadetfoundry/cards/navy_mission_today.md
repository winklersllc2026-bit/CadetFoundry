The Navy says it exists "to preserve the peace, respond in crisis and win decisively in war." It operates "from seabed to space," in cyberspace and the information environment.

What that means day to day, in the Navy's own examples:
- **Ships** keep shipping lanes open, so goods can cross the ocean without interruption.
- **Submarines** defend the fiber-optic cables on the sea floor that carry the internet.
- **Naval aviators** keep the skies clear and deliver strikes when needed.
- **Cyber teams** defend the nation's networks.

The modern Navy has hundreds of ships — including aircraft carriers, submarines and surface ships — and thousands of aircraft. Sailors have contributed to advances in nuclear propulsion, radar, sonar and computing.
