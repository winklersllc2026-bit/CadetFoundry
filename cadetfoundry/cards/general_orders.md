title: The Three General Orders
topic: Basic Knowledge
source: Earlier editions of TC 7-21.13, The Soldier's Guide (not in the 17 September 2025 edition; the guard duty manual is no longer listed as active on armypubs)
---
1. I will guard everything within the limits of my post and quit my post only when properly relieved.
2. I will obey my special orders and perform all my duties in a military manner.
3. I will report violations of my special orders, emergencies, and anything not covered in my instructions to the commander of the relief.

General orders apply to every Soldier on guard. Special orders are the extra instructions for one specific post.

**Check the wording with your drill sergeant** — the current Soldier's Guide no longer prints the General Orders, and no current public manual does, so Cadet Foundry can't confirm this is today's exact wording.

## Quiz
Q: Recite General Order Number 1. | A: I will guard everything within the limits of my post and quit my post only when properly relieved.
Q: Recite General Order Number 2. | A: I will obey my special orders and perform all my duties in a military manner. | WRONG: I will report violations of my special orders, emergencies, and anything not covered in my instructions to the commander of the relief.; I will guard everything within the limits of my post and quit my post only when properly relieved.; I will obey all orders except those of the commander of the relief.
Q: Recite General Order Number 3. | A: I will report violations of my special orders, emergencies, and anything not covered in my instructions to the commander of the relief. | WRONG: I will guard everything within the limits of my post and quit my post only when properly relieved.; I will obey my special orders and perform all my duties in a military manner.; I will report violations only to my squad leader at the end of my shift.
Q: How many General Orders are there? | A: Three
Q: To whom do you report violations of your special orders? | A: The commander of the relief | WRONG: The first sergeant; The nearest Soldier; The company commander
