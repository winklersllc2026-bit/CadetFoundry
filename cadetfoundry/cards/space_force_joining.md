**Enlisted Guardians.** You need a high school diploma or GED. The steps are:
1. **Qualify**: be **17–42 years old**, a U.S. citizen, and earn a qualifying **ASVAB** score.
2. **Apply** through a recruiter.
3. **Test**: the ASVAB measures arithmetic reasoning, math knowledge, word knowledge and paragraph comprehension.
4. **Screen**: moral and physical standards, then a counselor helps you choose a job.
5. **Train**: wait in the **Delayed Entry Program (DEP)** until you leave for basic training.

**Basic Military Training (BMT).** Guardians go through the **same BMT as Air Force trainees, about 7.5 weeks**. They also get **21 hours** of Space Force classes covering emotional intelligence, Space Force structure, and briefings from senior leaders on military doctrine.

**College while you serve.** Enlisted Guardians are automatically enrolled in the **Community College of the Air Force (CCAF)** and start earning credit in basic training.

**Officers** need a college degree. There are three main paths:
- the **U.S. Air Force Academy**
- **Air Force ROTC** at more than 1,100 colleges
- **Officer Training School (OTS)**, for people who already have a degree
