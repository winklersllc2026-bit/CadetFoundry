**Attention** is the basic military position, and most other movements start from it.
- Heels together, with the feet turned out at **45 degrees**.
- Legs straight but not stiff. Hips and shoulders level, chest lifted.
- Arms straight, **thumbs along the trouser seams**, fingers in their natural curl.
- Head and body erect, eyes straight ahead, chin pulled in slightly.
- Stand still and do not talk.

**Four positions of rest**, all taken from attention:
- **Parade rest**: the left foot moves **12 inches** to the left and the hands are clasped behind the back, right hand inside the left. Silent and still.
- **At ease**: keep the right foot in place. You may move but must not talk.
- **Rest**: keep the right foot in place. You may move and talk quietly.
- **Fall out**: leave ranks but stay nearby.

From parade rest, at ease or rest, the only command you may receive is **"ATTENTION."**

**Facings** ("Right, FACE," "Left, FACE," "About, FACE") are **two-count** movements done at quick time. The arms stay at your sides.

**Eyes right (left)**: turn the head **45 degrees**. "Ready, FRONT" brings it back.

**Hand salute**: raise the right hand until the tip of the forefinger touches the headdress **above and slightly right of the right eye**. The fingers are joined, the upper arm is parallel to the deck, and the forearm is at **45 degrees**.
- Salute when the person or colors are **six paces** away, or at their nearest point if they won't come that close.
- Don't salute if they never come within **30 paces**.
- Hold the salute until it is returned or they have passed.
- "Ready, TWO" ends a salute given in formation.

## Quiz
Q: At attention, at what angle are the feet turned out? | A: 45 degrees
Q: Name the four positions of rest. | A: Parade rest, at ease, rest, and fall out | WRONG: Attention, parade rest, halt, and march; Sit, kneel, lie, and stand; At ease, relax, stand by, and dismissed
Q: What is the only command you may receive at parade rest? | A: ATTENTION | WRONG: FORWARD, MARCH; FALL OUT; RIGHT, FACE
Q: How far does the left foot move at parade rest? | A: 12 inches
Q: At what distance do you render a hand salute? | A: Six paces
Q: How many counts is "Right, FACE"? | A: Two
Q: What command ends a hand salute in formation? | A: Ready, TWO
