**Keeping money in a bank or credit union** is safer than keeping cash at home.

**FDIC deposit insurance** protects your money in deposit accounts at **FDIC-insured banks** if the bank fails. It covers at least **$250,000 per depositor, per ownership category, at each insured bank.**
- **Covered:** checking accounts, savings accounts, and **certificates of deposit (CDs)**.
- **Not covered:** stocks, bonds, mutual funds and **crypto assets**.

**Words to know** (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau):
- **Overdraft** — when you don't have enough money in your account to cover a purchase, but the bank pays it anyway. Many banks **charge a fee** for this.
- **NSF (non-sufficient funds) fee** — a fee that can happen when a payment from your account is **not paid** because there isn't enough money.
- **Certificate of deposit (CD)** — a special type of savings account at a bank or credit union.
- **Joint account** — an account shared by more than one person; generally everyone on it can withdraw money.

**Avoid fees:** track your balance (your budget helps), and ask your bank how its overdraft coverage works before you open an account.
