**Four signs it's a scam:**
1. **They pretend to be someone you know** — a government agency or a familiar company. They can even fake the caller ID.
2. **They say there's a problem or a prize** — you owe money, your account has a problem, or you "won" something but have to pay first.
3. **They pressure you to act right now** — sometimes with threats — before you can check their story.
4. **They tell you to pay a specific way** — gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers or payment apps.

**How to protect yourself:**
- Don't give personal or financial information to a request you didn't expect.
- Don't click their links or call the number they give you — look up the real company yourself.
- Take your time. Real businesses give you time to decide.
- Never pay someone who insists on gift cards, crypto, a wire service or a payment app, and never deposit a check and send money back.
- **Stop and talk to someone you trust** — a parent, your instructor, a friend.
