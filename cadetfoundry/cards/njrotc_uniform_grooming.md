**Smartness** (Ch. 1, B.1): wearing the Navy uniform "should be a matter of personal pride." Caps are worn squarely on the head with the bottom edge horizontal; shoes are shined and in good repair; no cell phones, pens, jewelry or similar items worn exposed.

**Wearing the uniform** (Ch. 1, C):
- NJROTC Cadets wear the uniform **at least one full school day each week**, plus drills, ceremonies and parades.
- **Outdoors, stay covered** (cap on) unless ordered to uncover.
- **No partial uniforms** — you can't "save" parts of it for inspection.
- Never wear it for political activities, private jobs or commercial interests, or anywhere it would discredit NJROTC or the Navy.
- The **uniform of the day** is whatever your naval science instructor directs.

**Grooming** (Ch. 1, D) — a few of the standards:
- Hair neat, clean and groomed; it must not interfere with wearing covers or safety equipment. Bizarre or faddish styles aren't authorized.
- Men: sideburns no lower than the middle of the ear; mustaches neat and closely trimmed; no other facial hair; nails not past the fingertips.
- Women: hair may touch but not fall below the lower edge of the back of the collar; bulk no more than 2 inches (bun 3 inches); nails no longer than ¼ inch.
- One ring per hand; one watch and one bracelet; necklaces not visible. Sunglasses are never worn in formation.

**Insignia** (Ch. 1, G): **service stars** above the left pocket show years completed in NJROTC. **Ribbons** go in rows of three, ¼ inch above the left pocket. Earned **aiguillettes** (cords) go on the left shoulder, except the Leadership Academy aiguillette on the right.
