The Marine Corps values are **honor, courage, and commitment** — the same three words as the Navy's core values. The Marine Corps calls them "the code that guides every decision, every mission, every moment in uniform."

- **Honor** — "the foundation of a Marine's character": honesty, accountability and a firm moral compass — doing what is right "even when no one is watching."
- **Courage** — "isn't the absence of fear—it's the will to face it": standing up for what's right and continuing when others would give up.
- **Commitment** — "a promise—to the Corps, to their fellow Marines, and to the mission": striving for excellence, refusing to quit, and never letting those around you down.

**Semper Fidelis** — Latin for **"Always Faithful"** — is the official motto of the Marine Corps, established in **1883**. Marines use it as a greeting and as an expression of loyalty to each other and to the country.
