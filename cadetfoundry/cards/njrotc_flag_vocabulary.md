**Parts of a flag:**
- **Union (canton)**: the upper corner nearest the staff. On the U.S. flag it is the blue field with stars.
- **Hoist**: the edge nearest the staff. As a verb, "hoist" means to raise a flag.
- **Fly**: the edge farthest from the staff.
- **Halyard**: the rope used to raise a flag.

**Kinds of flags:**
- **Ensign**: a national flag flown by ships. The U.S. flag serves as the national flag, the naval ensign and the civil ensign.
- **Jack**: a flag flown at the **bow** of a warship at anchor. The U.S. Union Jack is 50 white stars on a blue field.
- **Colors**: flags carried by people on foot, such as a color guard.
- **Storm flag**: a smaller U.S. flag flown in bad weather.
- **Garrison flag**: a large flag flown at forts. The Star-Spangled Banner over Fort McHenry was **30 by 42 feet**.

**Display customs:**
- The flag flies from **sunrise to sunset**. It may stay up 24 hours if it is properly lit at night.
- On **Memorial Day** it flies at **half-staff until noon**.
- **Half-staff** means halfway between the top and the bottom of the staff.
- A worn-out flag is destroyed in a dignified way, **preferably by burning**.

**Pledge of Allegiance.** It is **not recited in military formations**. At events with civilians, Cadets in uniform outdoors stand at attention, face the flag and salute without speaking. Indoors they stand at attention without saluting, and saying the Pledge is optional.

## Quiz
Q: What is the union (canton) of the U.S. flag? | A: The blue field with stars in the upper corner nearest the staff | WRONG: The red and white stripes; The edge farthest from the staff; The rope used to raise a flag
Q: What is the halyard? | A: The rope used to raise a flag | WRONG: The blue field with stars; The edge farthest from the staff; The ornament on top of the pole
Q: What is the fly of a flag? | A: The edge farthest from the staff | WRONG: The edge nearest the staff; The blue field with stars; The rope used to raise a flag
Q: Where is the jack flown on a warship? | A: At the bow, when anchored | WRONG: At the stern, when underway; On the mast, during battle; On the bridge, at night
Q: How is a worn-out flag properly retired? | A: Destroyed in a dignified way, preferably by burning | WRONG: Thrown in the trash; Cut into rags; Folded and kept forever
Q: How long is the flag at half-staff on Memorial Day? | A: Until noon | WRONG: All day; Until sunset; For one hour at dawn
Q: What does a Cadet in uniform outdoors do during the Pledge of Allegiance? | A: Stand at attention, face the flag, stay silent and salute | WRONG: Stand at attention with a hand over the heart and say the Pledge; Stand at parade rest and say the Pledge; Remove the cover and say the Pledge
