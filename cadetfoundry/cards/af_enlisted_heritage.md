The museum calls the enlisted force "the foundation" of Air Force operations. **NCOs are the backbone.** Their daily leadership "binds the force together like glue."

**Enlisted milestones:**
- **1907**: Corporal Eddie Ward becomes the first enlisted Airman.
- **1912**: Corporal Vernon Burge becomes the first enlisted pilot.
- **1941**: the first Black enlisted trainees join the Army Air Forces. They become the **Tuskegee Airmen**.
- **1947**: the Air Force becomes an independent service.
- **1949**: Air Force basic training ends racial segregation.
- **1950**: the title **"Airman"** is established for enlisted members.
- **1967**: **Paul W. Airey** becomes the first **Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force**.
- **1972**: the **Community College of the Air Force** is created.
- **2020**: JoAnne S. Bass becomes the first woman to serve as Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force. The title **"Guardian"** is created, and **Roger A. Towberman** becomes the first Chief Master Sergeant of the Space Force.

**Key enlisted leaders:**
- **First Sergeant ("first shirt")**: a special duty held by a senior NCO, not a rank. The first sergeant looks after the health, morale and welfare of everyone in the unit, and links officers and enlisted.
- **Chief Master Sergeant ("Chief")**: the highest enlisted rank. Chiefs provide strategic leadership.
- **Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force**: leads the enlisted force and advises the Secretary and the Chief of Staff of the Air Force.

**Part-time Airmen.** Most Guard and Reserve members serve close to home, **one weekend a month plus two weeks a year**. When not federally mobilized, Air National Guard units answer to their state **governor**.
