**The Pledge of Allegiance** (4 U.S.C. § 4):

> I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one Nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

**How to say it:** stand at attention facing the flag with your **right hand over your heart**. Men not in uniform remove any non-religious hat with the right hand and hold it at the left shoulder, hand over the heart. People **in uniform stay silent, face the flag and salute.**

**The national anthem** is **"The Star-Spangled Banner"** (36 U.S.C. § 301).
- **In uniform:** salute at the **first note** and hold it until the **last note**.
- **Everyone else:** face the flag, stand at attention, right hand over the heart.
- **No flag in sight?** Face the music and act as if the flag were there.
- Veterans and service members not in uniform may also salute.

**Respect for the flag** (4 U.S.C. § 8):
- It should **not be dipped** to any person or thing.
- It is flown **union down** (upside down) only as a signal of dire distress — extreme danger to life or property.
- It should **never touch anything beneath it** — the ground, the floor, water or merchandise.
- It should never be used as clothing, bedding or drapery.
- A flag too worn to display should be **destroyed in a dignified way, preferably by burning.**
