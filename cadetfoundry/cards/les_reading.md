**"Your pay is your responsibility."** Verify and keep your LES each month. Your most recent LES is on **myPay**, 24 hours a day. If your pay changes a lot and you don't understand why, ask your **finance office**.

**The money (blocks 10–22):**
- **10 Entitlements** — the pay and allowances you're getting (up to 15; extras go in Remarks).
- **11 Deductions** — taxes, SGLI, mid-month pay, dependent dental and so on.
- **12 Allotments** — money sent to savings or checking accounts, insurance, bonds. Reserve and National Guard members don't have allotments.
- **13 AMT FWD** — unpaid pay from your last LES. **14 TOT ENT**, **15 TOT DED**, **16 TOT ALMT** — the totals.
- **17 NET AMT** — amount forward, plus entitlements, minus deductions and allotments.
- **18 CR FWD** — unpaid pay carried to your next LES.
- **19 EOM PAY** — what you'll actually be paid on end-of-month payday.

**Leave (blocks 25–32):**
- **26 ERND** — leave earned; normally goes up **2.5 days each month**.
- **28 CR BAL** — your current leave balance.
- **29 ETS BAL** — the leave you're projected to have at your ETS.
- **32 USE/LOSE** — leave you'll **lose** if you don't take it this fiscal year.

**Taxes (33–49):** federal income tax, FICA (Social Security and Medicare) and state tax — this period and year to date. **44 ST** is the state you elected.

**Other blocks:** **6 ETS** is your Expiration Term of Service (YYMMDD). **4 PAY DATE** is your Pay Entry Base Date. **63 BASE PAY RATE** is the percentage of base pay you put into the TSP; **72** shows your TSP contributions for the year. **76 Remarks** explains starts, stops and changes to your pay.

Your LES shows your Social Security Number — don't share a photo of it.

## Quiz
Q: Where can you see your most recent LES at any time? | A: myPay | ALSO: where do I find my LES; how do I get my pay stub | WRONG: Military OneSource; Your unit's bulletin board; The VA website
Q: Which LES block shows what you'll actually be paid at the end of the month? | A: Block 19, EOM PAY | WRONG: Block 28, CR BAL; Block 63, BASE PAY RATE; Block 32, USE/LOSE
Q: Which block shows your current leave balance? | A: Block 28, CR BAL | WRONG: Block 19, EOM PAY; Block 32, USE/LOSE; Block 63, BASE PAY RATE
Q: How much leave do you normally earn each month? | A: 2.5 days | ALSO: how many leave days do I get a month; how much leave do soldiers get
Q: What does USE/LOSE (block 32) show? | A: Leave you'll lose if you don't take it in the current fiscal year | WRONG: Money you'll lose if you don't spend it this month; Equipment you must turn in or pay for; Leave you already used this year
Q: What is block 29 on the LES? | A: ETS BAL — the leave balance you're projected to have at your Expiration Term of Service (ETS) | WRONG: EOM PAY — what you'll be paid at the end of the month; CR BAL — your current leave balance; USE/LOSE — leave you'll lose this fiscal year
Q: What does ETS BAL (block 29) show? | A: Your projected leave balance at your Expiration Term of Service | WRONG: Your current leave balance; Your end-of-month pay; Your Thrift Savings Plan balance
Q: Where are allotments listed, and do Reserve and Guard members have them? | A: Block 12 — Reserve and National Guard members don't have allotments
Q: Which block shows the percentage of base pay going to your TSP? | A: Block 63, BASE PAY RATE | WRONG: Block 19, EOM PAY; Block 28, CR BAL; Block 32, USE/LOSE
Q: Where does the LES explain starts, stops and changes to your pay? | A: Block 76, Remarks
Q: Who do you ask if your pay changes and you don't understand why? | A: Your finance office | WRONG: A friend in another unit; A payday lender; Nobody — it will fix itself
