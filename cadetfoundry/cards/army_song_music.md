**The Army song** is **"The Army Goes Rolling Along."** It concludes all reviews, parades and honor guard ceremonies. When it plays, **stand at attention and sing the lyrics**. Stand at attention for the official songs of the other Services too (AR 600-25, para 4-6). It was dedicated by the Secretary of the Army on **Veterans Day, 11 November 1956**, and officially announced on 12 December 1957 (TC 3-21.5, para 1-13).

The lyrics are published by The U.S. Army Band (usarmyband.com), as AR 600-25 directs.

**The national anthem**, "The Star-Spangled Banner," became the national anthem by law on **3 March 1931**. Francis Scott Key wrote it after watching the flag still flying over Fort McHenry after a 25-hour British bombardment (TC 3-21.5, para 1-12). When it plays you stand at attention and sometimes render the hand salute (para 1-11).

**Bugle calls** (para 1-14) — five classes: warning, formation, alarm, service and ceremonial. Some you'll hear:
- **First Call** — get ready to assemble for a formation.
- **Reveille** — wake up for morning roll call; goes with raising the national Color.
- **Retreat** — honors the flag as it is lowered in the evening; **To the Color** plays right after it.
- **Mess Call** — mealtime. **Adjutant's Call** — the adjutant is about to form the unit. **Attention** — you're about to be called to attention.
