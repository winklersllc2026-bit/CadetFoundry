**Form W-4** — when you start a job, you fill out a W-4. The information on it helps decide **how much income tax your employer withholds** (takes out) from each paycheck.

**Students aren't automatically exempt** from withholding. If you work only part time or in the summer, you may qualify to be exempt — but an exemption is good for **only 1 year**, and you must give your employer a **new W-4 by February 15** each year to keep it.

**Social Security and Medicare** — your employer usually must withhold these from your pay even if you don't earn enough to owe income tax.

**Tips are income.** If you get **$20 or more in tips in a month** from one employer, you must report them to that employer.

**Babysitting, mowing lawns, your own small business** — that's **self-employment**. Everyone, **including minors**, must file a tax return if their **net self-employment earnings are at least $400**. If teens owe taxes, they should **file their own return** — there's no minimum age to sign one.

**Keep records and receipts.** Some work expenses can be deducted to lower what you owe.

**Why file even if you don't have to?** If income tax was withheld from your pay, filing a return is how you **get it refunded**.
