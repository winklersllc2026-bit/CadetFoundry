You are never alone, and asking for help is a strength.

- **In crisis or thinking about suicide**: call **988, then press 1** (Veterans & Military Crisis Line), or text **838255**. Available 24/7, and it serves current service members too.
- **Tell someone now**: your battle buddy, drill sergeant, chaplain, or any leader.
- **Chaplain**: confidential to talk to, whatever your faith or none.
- **Behavioral Health**: every installation has it; seeking help is normal.
- **Sexual assault or harassment**: your unit's SHARP representative, or the **DoD Safe Helpline 877-995-5247** (24/7, anonymous). You can choose a **restricted** report (confidential care, no investigation) or **unrestricted** (investigation).
- **Military OneSource**: **800-342-9647** — free confidential counseling, money help, and family support.
- **Legal trouble**: Trial Defense Service (free military defense lawyers).
