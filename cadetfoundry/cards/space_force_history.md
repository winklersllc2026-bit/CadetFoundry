The Space Force is new, but military space work is decades old:

- **1954** — the Western Development Division, the U.S. Armed Forces' first dedicated space organization, is established under **General Bernard Schriever**.
- **1958** — **Explorer 1**, the first U.S. satellite to carry science instruments, launches from Cape Canaveral.
- **1978** — the Air Force puts the first **Navstar/GPS** satellite into orbit.
- **1982** — **Air Force Space Command** is activated: launch, satellite control, missile warning and space surveillance.
- **1983** — Air Force Space Command adopts the slogan **"Guardians of the High Frontier"** — where the name **Guardians** comes from.
- **1991** — space forces prove critical in Operation Desert Storm.
- **2019** — the National Defense Authorization Act reclassifies Air Force Space Command as the **U.S. Space Force**, the sixth military branch. **General John Raymond** becomes the first Chief of Space Operations.
- **2020** — 86 Air Force Academy graduates become the Space Force's first commissioned officers from the Academy.
