**Be, Know, Do.** The Army's **leadership requirements model** (ADP 6-22, used throughout FM 6-22) sets the expectations for every leader — military or civilian, officer or enlisted, active or reserve (para 1-12):
- **Attributes** are what the Army wants leaders to **be and know** — internal characteristics.
- **Competencies** are what the Army wants leaders to **do** — skills and behaviors.

**Three attribute categories**
- **Character** (para 2-26) — a person's true nature: morals, virtues, values. Leaders of character show: **Army Values, empathy, Warrior Ethos/Service Ethos, discipline, humility**.
- **Presence** (para 2-27) — the impression a leader gives through appearance, demeanor, actions and words: **military and professional bearing, fitness, confidence, resilience**.
- **Intellect** (para 2-28) — brainpower and knowledge to understand situations, judge, solve problems and act: **mental agility, sound judgment, innovation, interpersonal tact, expertise**.

**Three competency categories**
- **Leads** (para 2-29) — providing purpose, direction and motivation. Five competencies: **leads others, builds trust, extends influence beyond the chain of command, leads by example, communicates**.
- **Develops** (para 2-30) — **creates a positive environment/fosters esprit de corps, prepares self, develops others, stewards the profession**.
- **Achieves** (para 2-31) — **gets results**: tasks and missions done on time and to standard.
