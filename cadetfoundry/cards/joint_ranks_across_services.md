**Pay grade is not rank.** Pay grades such as E-1, W-2 and O-5 sort pay the same way across every service: **E** is enlisted, **W** is warrant officer, **O** is commissioned officer. The **rank** is the title — and it differs by service.

**The same pay grade, different titles** (E-4 as an example):
- Army — Corporal or Specialist
- Marine Corps — Corporal
- Navy and Coast Guard — Petty Officer Third Class
- Air Force — Senior Airman
- Space Force — Specialist 4

**Two ranks, one pay grade:** some grades hold two ranks with the same pay but different jobs. In the Army, a Corporal outranks a Specialist; in the Marine Corps, a Sergeant Major outranks a Master Gunnery Sergeant.

**What the job is called:** Army and Marine Corps — **military occupational specialty**; Navy — **rate**; Air Force — **Air Force specialty**.

**Officer groups:** the Army, Air Force, Space Force and Marine Corps say company grade (O-1 to O-3), field grade (O-4 to O-6) and general officers (O-7 and up); the Navy says junior grade, mid-grade and flag officers. A **Captain** is an O-3 in the Army, Air Force, Space Force and Marine Corps, but an **O-6** in the Navy and Coast Guard.

**Top enlisted of each service:** Sergeant Major of the Army, Sergeant Major of the Marine Corps, Master Chief Petty Officer of the Navy, Chief Master Sergeant of the Air Force, Chief Master Sergeant of the Space Force, Master Chief Petty Officer of the Coast Guard.

The **Coast Guard** is part of the Department of Homeland Security in peacetime and the Navy in time of war; its insignia match the Navy's except for color.
