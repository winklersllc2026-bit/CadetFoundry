**Enlisting in the Navy** (navy.com):
- **Age 17 to 41.** Under 18 needs **parental consent**.
- A **high school diploma or GED**.
- A **qualifying ASVAB score**.
- A **U.S. citizen or legal permanent resident**.
- Navy boot camp is at **Recruit Training Command, Great Lakes, Illinois**.

**Becoming a Navy officer:**
- A **four-year degree** by the time you're commissioned, and **U.S. citizenship** at commissioning.
- Officer candidates take the **Officer Aptitude Rating (OAR)** and/or the **Aviation Selection Test Battery (ASTB)**.
- **Age limits vary by program** — check with a recruiter.

**The officer paths:**
- The **U.S. Naval Academy** or **Navy ROTC (NROTC)**.
- **Officer Candidate School (OCS)** for college graduates — **13 weeks in Newport, Rhode Island**.
- **Officer Development School (ODS)** for professionals — **five weeks in Newport, Rhode Island**.
- **Limited Duty Officer (LDO)** — for enlisted Sailors with extensive technical skills.

Requirements change — confirm them on navy.com or with a recruiter.
