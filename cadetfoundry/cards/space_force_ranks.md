**Enlisted Guardians:**
- **E-1** Specialist 1 (Spc1)
- **E-2** Specialist 2 (Spc2)
- **E-3** Specialist 3 (Spc3)
- **E-4** Specialist 4 (Spc4)
- **E-5** Sergeant (Sgt)
- **E-6** Technical Sergeant (TSgt)
- **E-7** Master Sergeant (MSgt)
- **E-8** Senior Master Sergeant (SMSgt)
- **E-9** Chief Master Sergeant (CMSgt)

The top enlisted Guardian is the **Chief Master Sergeant of the Space Force (CMSSF)**.

**Compared with the Air Force.** The Space Force uses **"Specialist"** for E-1 through E-4, where the Air Force uses Airman Basic, Airman, Airman First Class and Senior Airman. At **E-5** a Guardian is a **Sergeant**, while an Airman is a Staff Sergeant. From E-6 up, the titles match.

**Officers** use the same titles as the Air Force:
- **O-1** Second Lieutenant
- **O-2** First Lieutenant
- **O-3** Captain
- **O-4** Major
- **O-5** Lieutenant Colonel
- **O-6** Colonel
- **O-7** Brigadier General
- **O-8** Major General
- **O-9** Lieutenant General
- **O-10** General

The Space Force has **no warrant officers** and **no wartime five-star rank**.

**Rank vs. paygrade.** "E-5" is a **paygrade** used to keep pay the same across services. "Sergeant" is the **rank**. The DoD page reminds readers that rank is "a badge of leadership," and responsibility grows with each step.
