Every step and march that begins from a halt starts with the **left foot**. The one exception is right step.

**Cadence and step length:**
- **Quick time**: **112 to 120 steps per minute** with **30-inch** steps. This is the normal cadence for drill and ceremonies. Arms swing **6 inches to the front and 3 inches to the rear**.
- **Double time**: **180 steps per minute** with **36-inch** steps. Forearms are raised parallel to the deck and the hands make fists.
- **Slow time**: **60 steps per minute**, used for funerals only.
- **Half step** and **back step**: **15 inches**.
- **Side step** (right or left step): **12 inches**, given only from a halt.
- **Mark time**: march in place at quick time. The ball of the foot rises about 2 inches and the heel about 4 inches.

**Halt** takes **two counts** from quick time and **three counts** from double time. "Platoon, HALT" from quick time is given as the right foot strikes the deck.

**Spacing:**
- **Normal interval**: one arm's length, shoulder to shoulder.
- **Close interval**: the space from shoulder to elbow, with the left hand on the hip.
- **Distance between ranks**: **40 inches**.
- An individual's **front** is counted as 22 inches and **depth** as 12 inches.

**Formations:**
- **Rank**: troops **side by side**.
- **File**: troops **one behind the other**.
- **Column**: elements one behind the other.
- **Line**: elements side by side.

**Counting cadence.** On "Count Cadence, COUNT" the whole unit counts for 8 steps. On "Delayed Cadence, COUNT" the count ends with "United States Marine Corps."

## Quiz
Q: What is the cadence of quick time? | A: 112 to 120 steps per minute
Q: How long is a quick time step? | A: 30 inches
Q: What is the cadence and step length of double time? | A: 180 steps per minute, 36 inches
Q: When is slow time used? | A: For funerals only
Q: What is the distance between ranks in a formation? | A: 40 inches
Q: How many counts is a halt from double time? | A: Three
Q: Which foot do you step off with from a halt? | A: The left foot
