America's naval service began during the American Revolution. On **October 13, 1775**, the Continental Congress authorized a few small ships, creating the **Continental Navy**. That date is the Navy's birthday.

- **Esek Hopkins** was appointed commander in chief.
- **22 officers** were commissioned, including **John Paul Jones**, one of the Navy's most famous early captains.

The Marine Corps was created by the same Continental Congress less than a month later, on November 10, 1775 — the two services have served side by side ever since.
