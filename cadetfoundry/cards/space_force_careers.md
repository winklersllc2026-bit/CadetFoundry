The Space Force describes its work in three areas: **protecting satellites**, **humanitarian and military operations**, and **launches**.

**Why satellites matter.** Ask yourself what happens if GPS goes dark. The Space Force's answer: networks that use GPS, including TVs, navigation, cell phones and weather forecasts, couldn't do their jobs.

**Some Space Force careers:**
- **Space Systems Operator**: helps with rocket launches and space flight operations.
- **Targeting Analyst**: develops intelligence to analyze targets and assess threats.
- **Signals Intelligence Analyst**: pulls out and analyzes foreign communications from electromagnetic signals.
- **Cyberspace Effects Operations Officer**: plans and supervises cyberspace operations and satellite communications.
- **Acquisition Manager**: handles the logistics that make a launch succeed.

**Range safety.** Guardians make sure the area around a launch is safe for takeoff, and protect the launched spacecraft from collisions.

**Missile warning.** Ground and space-based systems watch for missile launches around the world.

**Getting there.** After Basic Military Training, enlisted Guardians go to **technical training**, listed at about **110 days**. A counselor gives placement options based on your strengths. The Space Force says most of those options work with **ground stations**.
