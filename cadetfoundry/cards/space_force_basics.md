**Birthday — December 20, 2019.** The Space Force was created when the National Defense Authorization Act for 2020 was signed into law — a stand-alone, **sixth branch** of the U.S. military and the first new branch since 1947. Its members are called **Guardians**.

**Mission:** "Secure our Nation's interests in, from, and to space."
- **In space** — space superiority, protecting the joint force and the nation from space and counterspace threats.
- **From space** — services like satellite communications, positioning, navigation and timing, and missile warning.
- **To space** — assured access to space through launch and range operations.

**Core values (2021):** **Character, Connection, Commitment, and Courage.**

**Ranks:** enlisted Guardians at E-1 to E-4 are **Specialist 1, 2, 3 and 4**; then Sergeant, Technical Sergeant, Master Sergeant, Senior Master Sergeant and Chief Master Sergeant. Officers use the same ranks as the Air Force.

Space Force JROTC units are run through Air Force JROTC.
