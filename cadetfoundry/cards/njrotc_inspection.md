A **personnel inspection** gives hard-working Cadets a chance to show "self-discipline, attention to detail, and pride" (Ch. 3, H).

**Forming for inspection** (Ch. 3, I): the company or platoon is the basic unit. On **PREPARE FOR INSPECTION**, the platoon commander commands **OPEN RANKS, MARCH**:
- First rank takes **two steps** forward.
- Second rank takes **one step** forward.
- Third rank stays in place.

The platoon commander aligns each rank, then reports to the inspecting officer: "First platoon is standing by for your inspection."

**The inspecting officer** moves from Cadet to Cadet, checks the front and then the rear of each rank, and finishes with remarks and an exchange of salutes. Then the platoon commander commands **CLOSE RANKS, MARCH** (Ch. 3, J).

**What inspectors look for** (Ch. 3, K) — first the overall impression, then the details:
- Good posture; cap clean and worn correctly
- Insignia and devices in the right place
- Face and hair groomed properly
- Uniform clean, pressed and fitted; shoes shined
- Ribbons earned, in the right order and position
- Aiguillette on the correct shoulder; no unauthorized jewelry

## Quiz
Q: What command does the platoon commander give to open ranks? | A: Open ranks, march
Q: When ranks open, how many steps forward does the first rank take? | A: Two
Q: When ranks open, what does the third rank do? | A: Stays in place | WRONG: Takes two steps forward; Takes one step backward; Faces about
Q: What does the inspecting officer check first? | A: The overall appearance of the Cadets | WRONG: Each Cadet's shoes; The guidon; The Cadets' haircuts
Q: Name two things an inspector checks on a Cadet's uniform. | A: Shined shoes and insignia in the right place | WRONG: The Cadet's grades and attendance; Cell phone and earbuds; The Cadet's school schedule and locker
