The **Guardian Commitment** gives each value three **"I will"** statements for team members and three for team leaders. Every Guardian, from the newest recruit to the most senior leader, is a **team member**.

**Connection… toward unity.** Team members say:
- "I will engage everyone with respect, honesty, and authenticity."
- "I will encourage my teammates to share their thoughts and talents."
- "I will give help when asked and seek assistance when I need it." The handbook adds that asking for help "is not a sign of weakness"; it is "a sign of strength and confidence."

**Commitment… to mastery.** Mastery is "a lifelong journey, not a destination." Team members say:
- "I will work to achieve audacious goals, learning from my experiences."
- "I will offer candid feedback when appropriate." Feedback should be constructive, never an excuse to judge.
- "I will embrace challenges and work to be better tomorrow than I am today." Mastery is measured **against yourself**, not your teammates.

**Courage… to be bold.** Guardians "do what needs to be done and say what needs to be said because it is right." Most courage is everyday courage: telling your boss something is missing, or saying "we need to slow down because we're not doing this properly." Team members say:
- "I will be steadfast and stand up for what is right."
- "I will seek opportunities to innovate."
- "I will share thoughts and ideas to help the team and the mission."

**Team leaders also promise** to:
- keep an environment where people can speak **without fear**;
- **seek feedback** on their own leadership;
- support their team "regardless of outcome," treating failure as a chance to learn.
