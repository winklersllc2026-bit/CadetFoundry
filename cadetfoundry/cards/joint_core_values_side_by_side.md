Every service names the values it expects its members to live by:

- **Army** — Loyalty, Duty, Respect, Selfless Service, Honor, Integrity, Personal Courage (they spell **LDRSHIP**)
- **Navy** — Honor, Courage, Commitment
- **Marine Corps** — Honor, Courage, Commitment
- **Air Force** — Integrity First, Service Before Self, Excellence In All We Do
- **Space Force** — Character, Connection, Commitment, Courage

**What they share:** every list asks for honesty and doing the right thing when no one is watching, putting the mission and the team ahead of yourself, and the courage to do what's right when it's hard. The words differ; the ideas line up.

**Think about it:** which value would help most in your next group project? Which one is hardest for you?
