**Who's in it** (para 15-13): **two (or three) sergeants and two specialists or privates.** It is an **honor** to be selected.
- The **senior sergeant — the Color sergeant — carries the national Color** and **commands the Color guard**, giving the commands for movements and honors.
- Color guards wear the same uniform as the other troops, plus white dress gloves, neck scarves and a flag carrier (harness).

**How it marches** (para 15-15)
- In **one rank at close interval**, with the **Color bearers in the center**.
- The Color guard **does not do Rear March or About Face.**
- It changes direction by **wheeling**: ***Right (Left) Wheel*, MARCH.** The member nearest the turn is the pivot and marches in place while turning; the others shorten their steps and turn in an arc to stay aligned.

**Passing in review** (para 15-16): on ***Eyes*, RIGHT**, the organizational Color salutes; ***Ready*, FRONT** returns it to the Carry. **The guard on the right flank does not do Eyes Right.**

**Which Colors dip?** (para 15-12) The **organizational Color** (including the U.S. Army flag) dips in salute during the national anthem, "To the Color," and when honoring certain senior officials. **The U.S. flag is never dipped.** When marching, organizational Colors salute **six steps** before the person being honored and return to the Carry six steps after.

**Resting** (para 15-18): during a ceremony when units are at At Ease, the Color guard is at **Parade Rest**. Color bearers keep the staffs **vertical**.

## Quiz
Q: Who is in an Army Color guard? | A: Two or three sergeants and two specialists or privates | WRONG: Four officers; Two privates and two officers; One sergeant and four drummers
Q: Who carries the national Color and commands the Color guard? | A: The senior sergeant — the Color sergeant | WRONG: The junior private; The rifle guard on the right flank; The platoon leader
Q: How does a Color guard change direction? | A: By wheeling — Right or Left Wheel, MARCH | WRONG: By Column Right, MARCH; By About Face; By Rear, MARCH
Q: Does the Color guard do About Face? | A: No
Q: Which member of the Color guard does not do Eyes Right? | A: The guard on the right flank | WRONG: The guard on the left flank; Every member of the guard; No one — all of them do
Q: Is the U.S. flag ever dipped in salute? | A: No — only organizational Colors dip
Q: How many steps before the person being honored does a marching organizational Color salute? | A: Six steps
