The Air Force's **Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA)** changed in 2026. Airmen take it **every six months**, and it's scored out of **100 points**:

- **Cardiorespiratory — 50 points:** a **2-mile run** or the **20-meter High Aerobic Multi-shuttle Run (HAMR)**.
- **Body composition — 20 points:** **waist-to-height ratio**.
- **Muscle strength — 15 points:** 1 minute of push-ups, or 2 minutes of hand-release push-ups.
- **Core endurance — 15 points:** 1 minute of sit-ups, 2 minutes of cross-leg reverse crunches, or a timed forearm plank.

**Timeline:** tests from March 1 to June 30, 2026 were practice ("diagnostic"); official testing under the new standards began **July 1, 2026**. Scores now appear in Airmen's performance reports.

Score charts and the rules (AFMAN 36-2905) are updated over time — check the official Air Force fitness program page for current numbers.
