**Officer Candidates School (OCS)** — a **10- to 12-week** program at **Quantico, Virginia**, often compared to "an officer's boot camp." Candidates are tested in three areas: **academic knowledge, physical fitness and leadership ability**.

- OCS motto: **Ductus Exemplo** — Latin for **"Lead by example."**
- **The Quigley** — a legendary OCS obstacle of flooded trenches, barbed wire and submerged tunnels.
- Those who meet the standard are commissioned as **Second Lieutenants**.

**The Basic School (TBS)** — every new Marine officer trains here together, whatever job they'll do later, to become able to lead Marines under any conditions. It starts with individual skills (rifle and pistol, land navigation, communications, combat lifesaving, Marine Corps Martial Arts) and moves on to rifle squad leadership and tactics.

**Lesson for Cadets:** in the Marine Corps, every officer — pilot, lawyer or logistics officer — first learns to lead a rifle squad.
