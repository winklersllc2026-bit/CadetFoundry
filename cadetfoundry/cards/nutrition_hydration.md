**A healthy eating pattern** (paras 8-5, 8-6): a variety of vegetables, fruits (especially whole), grains (at least half whole grain), low-fat dairy, varied protein (seafood, lean meat, poultry, eggs, beans, nuts), and healthy oils. Limit **added sugar** and **saturated fat** to under 10% of daily calories each, and **sodium** to under 2,300 mg a day.

**Calories per gram** (para 8-7): carbohydrate **4**, protein **4**, fat **9**, alcohol **7**.

**In Basic, fuel first** (para 8-24): trainees who struggle to have enough energy for the physical parts of BCT should focus on **fuel to perform**, not on losing weight.

**Before exercise** (paras 8-32, 8-33): carbohydrate-rich food and fluids 2–4 hours before. No time to eat before early PT? About 30 grams of easy carbohydrate an hour before — a banana, applesauce, or toast with peanut butter.

**After exercise** (para 8-36): within **30–60 minutes**, 50–100 g of carbohydrate and **15–25 g of protein** — for example a cup of low-fat chocolate milk, or yogurt and a piece of fruit.

**Hydration** (paras 8-37 to 8-40, table 8-3):
- Even mild dehydration (under 1% of body weight) slows thinking; **2%** seriously hurts mental function, mood and energy.
- Drinking only when thirsty restores just **some** of the fluid you lose.
- **Before:** at least 1–2 cups 2 hours before, and ½–1 cup right before.
- **During:** ½–1 cup every 15–20 minutes — but **never more than 1.5 liters (about 6 cups) an hour**.
- **After:** at least 1–2 cups.
- A little caffeine (under 200 mg) doesn't hurt hydration.

Hot weather has its own fluid and work/rest rules — follow your cadre and TB MED 507.
