Many interviews now happen by **video or phone**. CareerOneStop's four tips:

1. **Be tech-ready.** Check that your computer, camera and microphone work. Practice with the employer's video platform (such as Zoom or Teams).
2. **Do your research.** Learn the employer's mission and goals, review the job description, and practice answers with examples of your strengths.
3. **Set up your space.** A **quiet place with good lighting** and a clean, professional-looking background. **Mute notifications** and close doors.
4. **Do a practice run.** Have a friend mock-interview you, and use their feedback on your answers, eye contact and energy.

**Good to know**
- It takes **more effort to show enthusiasm** on video or phone — sit up straight, keep eye contact, nod and smile.
- Some employers ask for a **pre-recorded video**. A real person watches it later, so bring the same energy — and you may be able to re-record an answer before you submit.
- No computer or quiet space? Your local **American Job Center** or **library** may be able to help.
