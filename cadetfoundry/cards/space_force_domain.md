**The space domain** begins above the altitude where the atmosphere stops having a meaningful effect on objects. It is a harsh place: heavy radiation, crowded with debris, and drastic temperature swings.

**Three orbital regimes.** Each is a region where one source of gravity is in charge.
- **Geocentric**: Earth's gravity dominates. This covers the orbits around Earth, out to and beyond geosynchronous orbit.
- **Cislunar**: from geosynchronous orbit out to the Moon's neighborhood, shaped by the gravity of the Earth and the Moon.
- **Heliocentric**: the parts of the solar system governed by the Sun's gravity.

**Space operations also depend on:**
- the **electromagnetic environment**: radio waves, microwaves, light and other signals that carry communications and sensing;
- **cyberspace**: networks, computers and data. Every space operation runs through it;
- the **air, land and maritime domains**, where ground stations and user equipment such as GPS receivers are located.

**Three segments of every space system:**
1. **Orbital segment**: the satellites in space.
2. **Terrestrial segment**: the equipment on land, at sea and in the air.
3. **Link segment**: the signals that connect them.

An attack on **any one** segment can deny, degrade or destroy a space capability. Protecting a capability means protecting all three.
