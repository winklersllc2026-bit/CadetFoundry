**Two kinds of ASVAB scores** (goarmy.com):
1. **AFQT** (Armed Forces Qualification Test) — decides whether you can join. You need **at least 31** to enlist. With at least **21**, the academic track of the **Future Soldier Preparatory Course** (90 days) can help you raise it.
2. **Line scores** (category scores) — decide which **jobs** you qualify for.

**Ten subject areas:** General Science, Arithmetic Reasoning, Word Knowledge, Paragraph Comprehension, Mathematics Knowledge, Electronics Information, Auto and Shop Information, Mechanical Comprehension, Assembling Objects, and Verbal Expression.

**The line scores and the subjects they need high scores in:**
- **General Technical:** Verbal Expression and Arithmetic Reasoning (example job: 91S Stryker Systems Maintainer)
- **Clerical:** Verbal Expression, Arithmetic Reasoning, Mathematics Knowledge (42A Human Resources Specialist)
- **Combat:** Verbal Expression, Auto and Shop, Mechanical Comprehension (11B Infantryman)
- **Electronics:** General Science, Arithmetic Reasoning, Mathematics Knowledge, Electronics Information (94M Radar Repairer)
- **Field Artillery:** Arithmetic Reasoning, Mathematics Knowledge, Mechanical Comprehension (13U)
- **General Maintenance:** General Science, Auto and Shop, Mathematics Knowledge, Electronics Information (92R Parachute Rigger)
- **Mechanical Maintenance:** Auto and Shop, Mechanical Comprehension, Electronics Information (91P)
- **Operators and Food:** Verbal Expression, Auto and Shop, Mechanical Comprehension (92G Culinary Specialist)
- **Surveillance and Communication:** Verbal Expression, Arithmetic Reasoning, Auto and Shop, Mechanical Comprehension (17E)
- **Skilled Technical:** General Science, Verbal Expression, Mechanical Comprehension, Mathematics Knowledge (89B Ammunition Specialist)

**Retaking it:** wait one calendar month after the first try; the third try comes no earlier than six calendar months after the second. **Your most recent score counts — not your highest.**

Each job sets its own minimum line scores, and they change. Ask your recruiter or career counselor for the current minimum for the job you want.
