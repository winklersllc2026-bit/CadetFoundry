Version 2026-10-11 · Questions: **support@cadetfoundry.org**

## 1. What Cadet Foundry is — and isn't
- Cadet Foundry is a **supplemental learning and leadership tool**. Your instructor, your program's curriculum and official sources come first.
- It is **not an official** JROTC, Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, Coast Guard, Department of Defense, school or government product, and **no one has endorsed it**.
- It **doesn't replace** your program's official systems, records, grading or the instructor's judgment. Its scores are for practice and feedback.
- It is **not a crisis or emergency service.** Its safety check shows help numbers (988, 911 and others) but **notifies no one.** If someone is in danger, call 911.

## 2. Free during the pilot
Cadet Foundry is free to use. If that ever changes, it will be announced in advance, and nothing you already have will stop working without notice.

## 3. Who may use it
- An instructor may install Cadet Foundry and use it **within their own JROTC program**, including making copies for their own Cadets (for example with "Make more flash drives").
- **Please don't pass Cadet Foundry on to other instructors, programs or schools.** Ask them to email support@cadetfoundry.org instead, and they'll get their own clean copy — with no one else's data on it.
- Don't sell Cadet Foundry, rebrand it, or present it as your own or as official.

## 4. Your data stays with you
Cadet Foundry stores everything on the computer (or USB drive) it runs on. **The Cadet Foundry project does not receive, see or keep any student data.** The instructor's program and school decide how long records are kept and when they are deleted (Cadet Foundry's End pilot and Delete profile tools help). See the **Privacy notice** for details.

## 5. Optional online AI
Online AI is **off** unless the person who manages the device turns it on with their own key from an outside AI provider. If they do, ordinary Ask questions go to that provider under **its** terms — read them first, especially about minors' data. Leave it off if you're unsure.

## 6. Use it responsibly
- Keep PINs private, keep the Class PIN to instructors, and keep the computer's own security on (screen lock, updates, disk encryption such as BitLocker).
- Use made-up profiles, not real Cadets, for demonstrations and practice.
- Don't use Cadet Foundry to harass anyone, to collect information about people who haven't agreed to it, or for anything unlawful.

## 7. Content
